# Independent audit of the two-filtration transfer

PASS as a newly proved, scoped application of the primary graded-kernel lemma. It should not be described as the literal nonmonomial statement of primary Proposition5.10.

I checked the cached primary text `tmp/eprint-2056/paper.txt`, Lemma3.12 (p30) and Proposition5.10/proof (pp49--50). Lemma3.12 accepts arbitrary polynomial matrices with column/row costs and entry degree bounds. Proposition5.10 defines its Rloc(t) through homogeneous monomial support blocks, not through arbitrary filtered image spaces. Thus the filtered test in FILTERED_NONMONOMIAL_SCOPE.md is an extension derived from Lemma3.12; its legitimacy needs the supplied proof.

That proof works. At the origin the contact quotient is graded by total jet degree. For L=image(U), decreasing-degree coordinate pivots give an injective fixed coordinate projection with exactly dim(L intersect V<=q)-dim(L intersect V<=q-1) rows of degree q. Input-filtered image increments would not justify this assertion. X and Y0 translation preserve the actual full-prefix source U, so all translated images equal this same L after scalar extension. A column of jet degree q has challenge degree at most q-r in a selected target row of degree r. The fixed rows therefore satisfy Lemma3.12 directly, without challenge-dependent row reduction. Restricting columns to q<=H remains sound even if L was formed from the entire source: rows of degree>H vanish on the active image.

For d<=D, every degree(q-1) tail has weight at most D(q-1), no greater than the smallest possible degree-q weight q(D-1). Echelon pivots consequently preserve each basis vector's weight upon taking highest total-degree parts. At the boundary q=D there can be ties, but weighted initial forms remain independent over k[X]: take the greatest jet degree among surviving terms of maximum weight and then its first pivot. This proves equality with the full-prefix limiting source, not just containment. Scaling both jet variables preserves the local contact ideal and gives the target associated-graded limit; the image of the limiting source is a graded subspace of that limit. Hence every degreewise target-filtration rank can only decrease. The subsequent homogeneous monomial degeneration is covered by the previously audited theorem.

No arbitrary filtered-space extension or nonmonomial Eq64 claim follows. In a manuscript the correct description is a new finite row-test corollary of Lemma3.12, agreeing with Eq63 on homogeneous supports. Its exact filtered ranks are mathematical quantities; no existing implementation or formally exported nonmonomial rank routine has been checked or claimed.
