# The omitted pole at infinity

A polynomial witness N(U) of degree≤10 is a separate branch from the proper finite-pole family. Let ψ(U)=U³+aU+γ and N=A(ψ)+UB(ψ)+U²C(ψ), with degrees A,B≤3,C≤2. At a double fiber with omitted root t,

    B=Ct, A−w=C(t²+a).

At a full fiber B=C=0 and A=w. Therefore, for C≠0, use the norm

    F∞=B³+aBC²−(X−γ)C³.

It is nonzero: otherwise B/C would be a rational right inverse of the cubic ψ, impossible by degrees of rational maps. It has degree≤9, vanishes at double fibers, and vanishes to order≥3 at full fibers. Thus d+3f≤9 and f≤2. If two fibers are full, C=cH and B=Hℓ for a quadratic H and degℓ≤1. Dividing by H³ leaves ℓ³+ac²ℓ−c³(X−γ), nonzero of degree≤3, so d≤3. These are exactly the norm inequalities used in the finite-pole support classification. Pair differences with old lifted cubics have degree≤10 as well, provided N is a fresh candidate. Thus the same two support patterns apply, both for Paley and for orbit2, with their respective permitted full/empty pairs.

If C=0,B≠0, at most three fibers are full and every other fiber has at most one match, giving at most20. If C=B=0, N=A(ψ) reduces to a base cubic with at least seven base agreements. For the two archived exact-list-seven words this is one of the original seven candidates and is not a fresh witness. No generic-base-list assumption should be omitted when using this final reduction.

Let W interpolate the seven triple received values, and T,R interpolate Ct,Ct² there. Reconstruction is B=T and A=W+R+aC. Degree constraints give the six affine linear equations

    T4=T5=T6=0,
    R4=−W4, R5=−W5, R6=−W6.

This is a six-by-three gate. Preserve singular solution spaces and check actual quad agreements and freshness. If the resulting N has exact degree10, its projective value at infinity is nonzero while the seven old lifted cubics of degree≤9 have value zero, as needed for the proposed subsequent extension. A lower-degree solution requires separate handling rather than silently assuming that separation.
