# Research allocation after the September 17 strategic review

The user asked whether the project was pursuing the most promising
routes, rather than accumulating variations of familiar constructions.
Three independent reviews and the root's cross-check produced a material
change in priorities. This file records decisions, not a new score or an
assertion that every possible mathematical approach has been exhausted.

## First priority: the actual better.codes incumbent

The live repository was rechecked at commit
cdb451f13fdc6c84f5fe363e77ee13a89bd30974; the board still reads
68.11--116.13. Its lower-track proof uses three auxiliary sources and
actual moving-component degree sums. Our earlier 1100-fold deficit used
the public paper's weaker single-equation formulas. That calculation was
correct within its scope, but it was the wrong benchmark for deciding how
close the current certificate is to an improvement.

The new replay exactly reproduces all 27 interpolation sources. At the
68.12 target, unchanged parameters fail all 27 sufficient dimension gates.
A bounded local search has found two valid replacements for one source;
that is individual-gate progress, not a complete certificate. The active
next task is to replay the full 4,970-row, 16-triple receipt and determine
whether restoring interpolation feasibility preserves the geometric and
final error budgets. Follow only exact inequalities valid for every
required case; a favorable selected case is insufficient.

The fourfold upper-track coefficient-fiber deficit is now a reserve
route. There is no positive evidence for the missing concentration, and
more cosmetic variants of the same packet bank are low priority.

See STRATEGY_BETTER_CODES_2026_09_17.md and
../better_codes_current_lower_2026_09_17/README.md.

## Independent mathematical track: agreement first

One agent is testing the unnormalized two-branch Riccati family, where
solutions already have a domain-level agreement mechanism. The initial
triangular recurrence leaves one constant coefficient, with residuals
quadratic or linear in that coefficient. The question is whether this
forces only linearly many isolated labels and whether persistent curves
can be handled at the actual proximity threshold.

This deliberately allows a stronger upper-bound result, rather than
assuming the existing quadratic upper bound must be attained. A narrow
family lemma alone does not establish general first-order behavior; if
its argument has no broader scope, stop after a concise result. Further
method-cost converse refinements and solution counts without an agreement
mechanism are no longer priority work.

See STRATEGY_INTRINSIC_TIGHTNESS_2026_09_17.md.

## High-risk construction track: new sources, explicit feasibility gates

The prime-field compiler is largely ready. The missing ingredient is a
nearest bank with growing size and fixed positive agreement surplus on a
short domain. Distinct candidate mechanisms deserve tests; repeated
cyclic Dickson variants do not constitute a diversified portfolio.

A new general characteristic-zero theorem has already closed one proposed
branch: on mu_(sr), with prime r coprime to fixed s and a coset-constant
word, every noninvariant polynomial of degree D has at most D+s-1
agreements. Thus changing the four word values or increasing the rate
does not rescue that growing-orbit construction. See
../cyclotomic_dickson_word/PRIME_ORDER_COSET_WORD_OBSTRUCTION.md.

A bounded pilot of deliberately dependent noncyclic incidences used
24 sixteen-candidate subsets of the complete 210-candidate F41 nearest
bank. All failed the prescribed unramified first-order lift; each failure
has an independently checked obstruction certificate. Stop this subset
rule rather than expand the census. It does not exclude ramified lifts
or unrelated characteristic-zero incidence identities.

The root also tested the concrete elliptic symmetrized-translation idea
symbolically. Against a multiplication-by-h received word on full
m-torsion, two complementary bounds give O(m) agreements on Theta(m^2)
nodes. This proposal therefore does not warrant enumeration. Other
elliptic mechanisms remain open; they must first exhibit a favorable
pole, degree, and agreement budget.

The remaining high-risk construction work should seek an explicit
scalable dependent moment/incidence identity, or a genuinely arithmetic
exceptional-prime source. Before any large computation, write its exact
length, degree, agreement, field, and growing-list parameters. Reject
compositions that keep the list bounded or make the gap vanish.

See STRATEGY_PRIME_FIELDS_2026_09_17.md and
../elliptic_source_gate/SYMMETRIZED_TRANSLATIONS.md.

## Resource and evidence rules

Use the three available subagent slots for distinct mechanisms; the root
cross-checks assumptions, audits promising results, and integrates papers.
Rotate a slot when its explicit feasibility test fails. Broad strategy
reviews should lead to experiments or proofs, not become an indefinite
planning activity. Numerical jobs retain the 384 MiB / 60-second guard.

Keep the main ePrint manuscript current, but do not grow it merely because
another restricted obstruction was proved. Separate the main conceptual
results, useful companion theorems, and unsuccessful research notes.
No better.codes gain, prime-field fixed-gap superlinear theorem, or
intrinsic first-order tightness is currently claimed.
