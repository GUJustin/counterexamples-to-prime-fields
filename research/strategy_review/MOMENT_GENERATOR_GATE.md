# Dependent-moment generator: current status

September 17, 2026. No new positive scalable identity was found in this
review. This is an open construction task, not a newly proved barrier.

The precise target is a domain of N distinct algebraic numbers and
L->infinity subsets of size A, with common first m elementary symmetric
coefficients, where m>=cN and A-m=K is a valid code dimension. Their
monic locators share their leading m+1 coefficients; subtracting them
from a common monic degree-A received polynomial produces candidates
of degree <K. The degree-A received polynomial ensures that A is the
true agreement maximum. Thus this formulation builds the nearest
boundary into the desired identity.

The number m of common nonleading coefficients must remain linear in N.
The following elementary positive identities do not meet that goal:
Prouhet finite differences have logarithmic moment depth in their support
size; disjoint unions of full equal-degree fibers retain fixed depth
while their domain grows; multiplying all locators by a common factor
raises both locator degree and difference degree equally and does not
increase the number of canceled leading coefficients. Independent
products inherit precisely this loss of normalized margin.

A genuinely new proposal should therefore supply a dependent factor-
exchange identity whose iteration increases BOTH the number of different
subsets and the canceled-coefficient depth proportionally to total domain
size. Merely supplying a new two-subset Prouhet--Tarry--Escott identity,
or a larger finite seed, is insufficient. No such iteration is currently
in hand, so no numerical census was launched or positive construction
claimed. This branch should reopen when an explicit identity or an
external theorem supplies this missing step.
