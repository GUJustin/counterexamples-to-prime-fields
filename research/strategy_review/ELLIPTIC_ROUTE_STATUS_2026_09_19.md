# Elliptic route status after the arbitrary-label closure

September 19, 2026. This supersedes the proposed 3/7 torsion-quintic scan, not the broader research objective.

The user target remains a superlinear exceptional-challenge construction with far sources, over a prime alphabet or on a practically relevant extension-field domain, or a verified improvement to better.codes. None has been obtained in this research block.

## What changed the next action

The 3/7 torsion-multiple family has exactly ell*n distinct supports, but at most 2n distinct exceptional labels on a far-source line. This is an arbitrary-label theorem, not merely the failure of its natural norm formula. All subbanks are covered. The finite norm-label rank-182 certificate was independently rebuilt by elliptic group law and ordinary modular elimination. Both records are published in commit 1a71faa67abb5c9de598964b2d25664cbe7dc230.

No Plucker computation was launched. The proposed raw fixed-H full-rank target was also mathematically impossible: the canonical three-dimensional weighted-tag space forces a three-dimensional exterior-square kernel. The general bound makes the revised computation unnecessary for this particular family.

## Remaining routes with a concrete distinction

1. Independently varying quotient tags can supply more than order-n base pairs. An explicit two-parameter extension has signed extras P,6Q,-4P,2Q,3P-8Q, with Miller partial sums P+6Q and -3P+6Q as the two tags. Their quotient coefficient matrix has determinant 24. This is only an algebraic candidate: no additive common-line identity is known. Its diagonal P=Q is the closed family, so repeating the same norm-label test on the entire enlarged family would add nothing.
2. Six extra errors, as well as five, remain strictly above the full first-order agreement bound and below finite Johnson at the fixed elliptic n,k. Seven or more do not. Generic L(6O) norms offer one additional parameter; whether it gives a new shared-line identity is being examined. The fixed-base bound still allows at most seven labels per pair, so merely changing the extra roots on order-n pairs cannot work.
3. The general moving norm-quintic Padé system is explicitly reduced to algebraic equations. Positive dimension, native split roots, distinct labels, and source farness remain separate requirements. The equations are not a counterexample.

Increasing the extra budget proportionally to ell while keeping the same code dimension drops below the first-order target. Retuning the dimension needs new proofs and is not an improvement by itself. No rental or large scan is justified merely by the count of support parameters.
