## Ranked routes

1. **Optimize the existing lower-track moving-component proof.**
   `SubmissionLower/Solution.lean` imports `MovingFiberCertificate6811`
   and exports `ProtocolClaim6811 331206655 1073741824`. The inspected
   `MovingFiberProfile6811` combines three auxiliary interpolation
   sources, separating helper and leading-coefficient exceptions.
   `MovingFiberDegreeSum6811` bounds the actual sum of moving-coordinate
   degrees without multiplicity weighting. The first concrete target is
   its parameter catalog and active component cases, not the obsolete
   single-equation worst-case product. Plausible payoff: one centibit
   from a finite arithmetic/profile improvement. Cost: moderate source
   reconstruction plus exact rational optimization. Missing ingredient:
   either a better certified source triple for every active case or a
   smaller aggregate bound; a favorable isolated case is insufficient.

2. **Improve interpolation rank using genuinely different sources.**
   Coefficient-dependent supports, second-Hasse-derivative auxiliary
   equations, and global kernel dependencies are outside the audited
   first-derivative-only standard-support converse. Test a precise new
   support/local-kernel identity on small instances before optimizing
   huge parameters. Payoff could exceed rounding gains or change a
   limiting curve. Cost is high; missing lemma is a uniform rank upper
   bound valid for arbitrary received words, with characteristic guards.
   Random low ranks for selected words would not prove it.

3. **Use additional Taylor tails and whole-kernel component cuts.**
   A true degree-bounded polynomial has vanishing higher Taylor
   coefficients, unlike an arbitrary formal solution. Two independent
   explainers can still share a component, so an actual gcd, saturation,
   or dimension-of-multiples argument is required. Current submissions
   already use these ideas; the task is to find a missing joint constraint
   or smaller actual degree allocation, not merely invoke them again.
   Payoff: reduce the dominant regular-family budget. Cost: substantial
   algebraic proof. Stop if the proposed cut vanishes identically on the
   worst component and no dimension argument handles that branch.

4. **Construct a genuinely new upper-track single-word bank.**
   Mixed-scale supports or correlated coefficient banks might have a
   provably smaller signature image. A fixed-word list can use the
   existing constrained-list reduction without needing an injective
   rational-pencil parameterization. Missing lemma: a list of sufficient
   size on the exact Koala NTT domain at the necessary agreement count,
   plus the required suffix handoff. Cost/risk: high. The currently
   counted B1024 bank is a line bank, not a single-word list; padding
   cannot silently convert it. General-domain constructions and choosing
   a larger splitting prime do not meet this benchmark.

5. **Exploit the eight-row structure with a valid common-support argument.**
   This would require joint information beyond a scalar projection on
   the lower track, or a structured interleaved list on the upper track.
   Independent row banks do not multiply their agreement supports:
   simultaneous row agreement is an intersection. Nor does eight-row
   interleaving permit replacing the single affine challenge with eight
   independent parameters. Payoff is uncertain; cost is high. A usable
   experiment must first specify one joint support/constraint identity.
   No such unused identity has been found.

## Why the apparent factor-four upper-track target is misleading

The best failed packet guarantee is6.8579e16 versus2.7498e17 required.
That is a fourfold *concentration theorem about enormous coefficient
fibers*, not four missing witnesses. Exact product-key counts are
essentially uniform, rotations cancel in naive quotient counting, and
the scalar-normalized common-core line cannot merge product classes.
No evidence supports the missing joint concentration. Further small
template exclusions have low expected payoff. Circle arguments also
need the exact code equivalence: this NTT subgroup is fixed pointwise
by Frobenius, unlike the norm-one circle used by the M31 constructions.

Read and cache the pinned moving-fiber certificate, catalog, profile,
receipt and final arithmetic files. Reproduce its exact numerical
inequalities, recording the worst active cases. Then test the next
integer agreement threshold181275 (current threshold181284), using the
same proof rules and a documented finite neighborhood of source
parameters. A radius with effective agreement181274+1/4096 would
support68.12 if every required inequality were proved; this is a target,
not an attained certificate.

Stop the bounded numeric search if no complete case cover passes in
that documented neighborhood, or if parameter variation would require
an unproved geometric lemma. Record which inequality fails. Do not
keep reoptimizing the weaker ePrint formulas or claim numerical success

and full-radius conditions are checked. A passing arithmetic candidate
would then need the corresponding Lean proof and kernel verification.
