# Independent audit

PASS, 2026-09-19. Actual note and primary theorem checked.

Quadratic graph curves have degree≤2, three degrees of freedom, and multiplicity type two. Sheffer–Szabo–Zahl Theorem 1.3 in https://arxiv.org/html/1502.07003v4 gives the displayed n^(3/5+ε)L^(4/5)+n+L expression without a transverse-intersection assumption. Its real analogue, stated there as Theorem 1.2, has no ε loss.

For fixed c>0, once c√n exceeds twice the theorem constant, the final L term is absorbed. Either the remaining n term controls LT, yielding L=O(√n), or L^(1/5)=O(n^(1/10+ε)), yielding L=O(n^(1/2+5ε)). Setting ε=η/5 proves the stated complex consequence; the real bound is O(√n). Constants and onset can depend on c and ε, as recorded.

The consequence concerns characteristic-zero configurations preserving the incidences and distinctness assumptions. Extra incidences after reduction modulo a prime are not excluded; no quantitative prime threshold or prime-field incidence theorem follows. Attribution as an established-theorem corollary is correct.
