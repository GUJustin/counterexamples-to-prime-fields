\paragraph{The core attains the classical finite Johnson list bound.}
On the core domain of size $N_0=L(L-1)$, the maximum list size at
agreement $A=2(L-1)$ for degree-at-most-two polynomials is exactly $L$,
even when the received word is allowed to vary. Indeed, for any list
of size $\ell$, choose exactly $A$ matching coordinates per candidate
and let $r_x$ count their selected incidences at coordinate $x$.
Distinct quadratics coincide at at most two coordinates, so
\[
 \frac{\ell^2 A^2}{N_0}-\ell A
 \le \sum_x r_x(r_x-1)
 \le 2\ell(\ell-1).
\]
For $L>2$, this is the usual finite Johnson bound
\[
 \ell\le\frac{N_0(A-2)}{A^2-2N_0}=L.
\]
The displayed quadratic bank attains equality: every core coordinate
has exactly two matches and every pair of bank polynomials coincides
at exactly two core coordinates. This is an equality example for the
classical counting bound, not a stronger decoding upper bound; the
received-line construction uses additional coordinates to turn these
core candidates into distinct singleton exceptional challenges.
