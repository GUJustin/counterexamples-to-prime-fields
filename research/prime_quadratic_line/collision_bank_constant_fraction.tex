% Standalone proof fragment; prime-field dimension-three construction.
\subsection{A regular collision bank with a constant fraction of nearby labels}
\label{subsec:collision-bank-constant-fraction}

Allowing two bank pairs to share a squared intersection coordinate yields
an exactly regular core.  The two square roots can be assigned to the two
pairs separately.  Combined with the translated grid, this gives the
following different gap--alphabet tradeoff.

\begin{theorem}[Regular collision bank]
\label{thm:collision-bank-constant-fraction}
There is a sequence of primes $p$ and length-$n$, dimension-three
Reed--Solomon codes over $\mathbb F_p$, with received words $F,G$ and
integers $A<T=A+d$, such that
\[
 \operatorname{agr}_3(F)=\operatorname{agr}_3(G)
   =\operatorname{CA}_3(F,G)=A,
 \qquad n a_1(3/n)<A<T<\sqrt{2n}.
\]
At least $2^{-19}p$ distinct parameters
$\lambda\in\mathbb F_p\setminus\{0,1\}$ have a singleton list at
agreement threshold $T$ for $(1-\lambda)F+\lambda G$.  Moreover,
\[
 d=\Theta\!\left(\frac{n^{1/4}}{\log n}\right),\qquad
 p=\Theta\bigl(n^{5/4}\log n\bigr),\qquad
 \frac{T-A}{n}=\Theta\!\left(\frac{n^{-3/4}}{\log n}\right).
\]
The successful witness need not have exactly $T$ agreements.  At the
relaxed threshold $A$, every word on this affine line has a list of size
at most $L+1$, where $L=\Theta(\sqrt n)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Let an integer $H$ tend to infinity, and let $v_H$ be the number of
primes in $[H/2,H]$.  Put
\[
 t=\lfloor v_H/16\rfloor,\quad L=2t^2,\quad
 M=\lfloor L/8\rfloor,\quad Q_0=LHM.
\]
Thus $t=\Theta(H/\log H)$.  The prime number theorem in the fixed
progression $1\pmod4$ supplies a prime
\[
 4096Q_0<p<8192Q_0,\qquad p\equiv1\pmod4
\]
for all sufficiently large $H$.  In particular $p>H^4$ eventually.
Among the four quartic-character classes of the $v_H$ small primes,
one contains at least $4t$ primes.  Choose four disjoint sets of $t$
primes from this class, and form two disjoint complete bipartite graphs
$K_{t,t}$, each joining one numerator set to one denominator set.

Choose a reference prime $z$ in that class.  For every numerator prime
$a$ and denominator prime $b$, choose squares $\alpha_a,\beta_b$ with
\[
 \alpha_a^2=a/z,\qquad \beta_b^2=b/z.
\]
For each of the $L$ edges put $\theta_{ab}=\alpha_a/\beta_b$.
These are distinct squares and satisfy $\theta_{ab}^2=a/b$.
Unique factorization, after clearing denominators and using $p>H^4$,
shows that a product of two distinct edge parameters has at most two
unordered representations.  Two representations occur exactly for
the two diagonals of a rectangle in one component: their products agree
exactly by the coherent choice $\theta_{ab}=\alpha_a/\beta_b$.
Shared-vertex pairs have just one representation.  Indeed, equality
of squared products fixes the numerator and denominator prime
multisets; the only alternative pairing is that rectangle.

Use the quadratic bank $P_\theta(X)=\theta+X^2/\theta$.
A pair $\theta,\phi$ meets at $X^2=\theta\phi$.
For a product with one pair representation, assign both square roots
to that pair.  For a product with two representations, assign one
root to each pair.  At an assigned root set the received value to the
sum of its assigned parameters.  The two pair sums are different:
equal sum and product would give the same unordered parameters.
Further, a bank parameter $\eta$ matches the assigned value only if
$(\eta-\theta)(\eta-\phi)=0$.  Thus every core coordinate has exactly
two bank owners.

Each bank edge has $(t-1)^2$ opposite partners in rectangles, losing
one of its two intersection coordinates for precisely those partners.
Every bank word therefore has exactly
\[
 A=2(L-1)-(t-1)^2=3t^2+2t-3
\]
core matches.  The core has $N_0=LA/2=t^2A$ distinct coordinates.
Set $g=0$ there and let $f$ be the assigned word.
Any quadratic outside the bank has at most $L$ core matches, by
counting at most two roots against each bank word and two owners at
every matched coordinate.

Choose $u_0,v_0$ independently uniformly in $\mathbb F_p$.
For $1\le u,v\le M$, put $U=u_0+u$, $V=v_0+v$.
Retain pairs with $U,V\ne0$ and $V/U$ a square, choose one square
root by a fixed rule, retain one representative per ratio, and remove
all core ratios.  Let $X$ be the retained count.  Ratio collisions
and hits on a specified core ratio each have probability at most $1/p$:
they are nontrivial affine equations in the translation.  Consequently
\[
 \mathbb E X\ge M^2\frac{(p-1)^2}{2p^2}
 -\frac{\binom{M^2}{2}}p-\frac{M^2\binom L2}p
 =\left(\frac12-o(1)\right)M^2.
\]
Since $X\le M^2$, the event $X\ge M^2/4$ has probability at least
$1/3-o(1)$.

On the grid set $g(x)=1/U$ and $f(x)=c_0/U$, with $c_0$ chosen below.
The labels of a bank word satisfy
\[
 \lambda+c_0=\theta u_0+v_0/\theta
                 +\frac{au+bv}{b\theta}.
\]
Each bank's raw labels lie in an affine image of an integer interval
of size at most $K=3HM<p$.  Since $a,b$ are distinct primes at least
$H/2$, any integer value $au+bv$ has at most $1+2M/H\le3M/H$
representations.  Put $d=\lfloor M/(64H)\rfloor$.  On $X\ge M^2/4$,
each bank therefore has at least
\[
 \frac{M^2/4-K(d-1)}{3M/H}\ge\frac{HM}{16}
\]
labels with at least $d$ retained matches.

Let $C$ count cross-bank collisions among all raw interval labels.
Every such pair has collision probability $1/p$, because its
translation coefficients include the nonzero difference of its bank
parameters.  Hence
\[
 \mathbb E C\le\frac{\binom L2(3HM)^2}{p},\qquad
 \Pr(C>LHM/64)<\frac{288}{4096}<\frac13.
\]
For sufficiently large $H$ this event can be avoided simultaneously
with $X\ge M^2/4$.  Fix such a translation.  Removing all bank-label
pairs involved in cross-bank collisions removes at most $2C$ pairs.
There remain at least $LHM/32$ distinct rich labels, each with only
one possible bank owner even among the non-rich raw labels.

The union of raw bank labels has size at most $3LHM<p-2$.
Choose $c_0\ne0$ outside it.  A nonconstant quadratic has at most
$2M$ grid matches at any label, by fixing $U$; a nonzero constant has
at most $M$.  The only exception is the zero polynomial at
$\lambda=-c_0$, which matches the whole grid.  Discard that label.

Set $T=A+d$ and $n=\lfloor T^2/2\rfloor+1$.
Since $A=(3/2+o(1))L$ and $M\le L/8$, one has
$n>N_0+X$ for all sufficiently large members.  Add neutral coordinates
with $g=0$ and $f=x^3$, avoiding the old coordinates, zero, and all
roots of $x^3-P_\theta(x)$ for the bank.  There are at most $3L$
additional forbidden points, and $p\gg n$, so this is possible.
Banks gain no matches.  Every outsider gains at most three, and away
from the discarded label its total is at most $L+2M+3<A$.
Thus each retained rich label has a singleton threshold list.

Any label outside all shifted raw bank labels and $\{-c_0\}$ has
exact maximum agreement $A$.  There are at least two such labels;
choose them as the two endpoints.  Ordinary common agreement is
exactly $A$: a zero explaining direction restricts the intercept to
the core and neutral block, whose maximum is $A$, while a nonzero
quadratic direction has at most two matches there and at most $2M$
on the grid.  These bounds persist under the invertible change to
the chosen endpoints.  Their normalized affine mixtures enumerate
the same line bijectively.  The number of singleton parameters is at
least
\[
 LHM/32-1>p/2^{18}-1\ge p/2^{19}
\]
eventually.  The same outsider bound gives the relaxed list bound
$L+1$, allowing zero at its exceptional label.

Finally $n=\Theta(t^4)$, $d=\Theta(t^2/H)$, and
$p=\Theta(Ht^4)$.  Since $t=\Theta(H/\log H)$, these give all the
claimed scales.  Both $A/\sqrt n$ and $T/\sqrt n$ tend to $\sqrt2$, whereas
\[
 n a_1(3/n)\le\sqrt{3n/2}+(3n/8)^{1/4}.
\]
Thus $A>n a_1(3/n)$ eventually. The definition of $n$ also gives
$T^2<2n$ exactly.
\end{proof}

This construction has rate $3/n$ and a vanishing normalized source gap.
Its constant exceptional fraction and larger absolute gap do not imply
fixed-rate progress, a prescribed evaluation domain, or quadratic-order
exceptional-count tightness.
