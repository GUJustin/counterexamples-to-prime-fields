% Scope: bank-witnessed labels from one translated integer grid only.
\begin{proposition}[A gap ceiling for one translated grid]
\label{prop:translated-grid-gap-ceiling}
Let $p$ be an odd prime, $M\ge2$, and $p>2(M-1)^2$.
Let $\mathcal X\subseteq\{1,\ldots,M\}^2$, let
$\Theta\subseteq\mathbb F_p^*$, and for each $\theta\in\Theta$
fix $c_\theta\in\mathbb F_p$.  Assign to $(u,v)\in\mathcal X$
the bank label
\[
 \ell_\theta(u,v)=\theta u+\theta^{-1}v+c_\theta.
\]
If $B$ distinct labels each have some bank parameter whose fiber
contains at least $d\ge2$ points of $\mathcal X$, then
\[
 B\le \frac{4M^2}{d}
       \left\lfloor\frac{M-1}{d-1}\right\rfloor^2
   \le \frac{16M^4}{d^3}.
\]
Consequently, for $M=O(\sqrt n)$ and $B=\Omega(p)$ with $p\ge n$,
one necessarily has $d=O(n^{1/3})$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Suppose a fiber for $\theta$ contains at least $d$ points.
Every difference vector $(h,k)$ in that fiber satisfies
$\theta^2h+k=0\pmod p$.  The determinant of any two such
vectors is divisible by $p$ and has absolute value at most
$2(M-1)^2<p$, so it vanishes as an integer.  Thus the fiber
lies on one integer line with a primitive direction $(b,-a)$,
where $b>0$, $a\ne0$, and $\theta^2=a/b\pmod p$.
The integer parameters of its distinct points span at least $d-1$.
The coordinate widths are at most $M-1$, hence
\[
 0<|a|\le K,\qquad 1\le b\le K,
 \qquad K=\left\lfloor\frac{M-1}{d-1}\right\rfloor.
\]
There are at most $2K^2$ such signed ratios and at most two
square roots $\theta$ per ratio.  At most $4K^2$ bank parameters
can therefore have a qualifying fiber.  For each parameter the
fibers partition $\mathcal X$, so at most $M^2/d$ labels qualify.
This proves the first bound.  The second follows from
$d-1\ge d/2$.

For explicit constants, if $M\le C\sqrt n$ and $B\ge cp$,
then $d^3\le16C^4n^2/(cp)\le(16C^4/c)n$.
\end{proof}

Translations of the grid are absorbed into $c_\theta$, and arbitrary
deletions of grid points do not weaken the bound.  In the preceding
construction, a threshold-$A+d$ bank witness has at least $d$ grid
matches because its core agreement is at most $A$ and its neutral
padding agreement is zero.  Thus the proposition applies to its
successful bank-witnessed labels.  Its achieved
$d=\Theta(n^{1/3}/(\log n)^2)$ has the largest polynomial exponent
allowed by this one-grid bound.  At its actual field size
$p=\Theta(n(\log n)^4)$ the bound is
$d=O(n^{1/3}/(\log n)^{4/3})$.
This is a bound for the specified grid and bank mechanism; it does
not restrict arbitrary received words, other fresh-coordinate sets,
or labels witnessed only by polynomials outside the bank.
