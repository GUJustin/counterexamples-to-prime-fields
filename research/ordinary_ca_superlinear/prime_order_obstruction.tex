\subsection{Characteristic-zero obstructions at prime and prime-power cyclic orders}
\label{sec:ordinary-ca-prime-order-obstruction}

The following restriction concerns a characteristic-zero cyclic source;
it does not restrict exceptional split characteristics in which such a
source may exist only after reduction.
The prime-order statement also follows from the more general coset-word
argument recorded in
\path{research/cyclotomic_dickson_word/PRIME_ORDER_COSET_WORD_OBSTRUCTION.md}.
We give a direct proof that extends to the prime-power bound below.

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:ordinary-ca-prime-order-obstruction}
Let $r>5$ be prime, let $\mu_{4r}$ be the $4r$th roots of unity in an
algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, and put
\[
 W_r(X)=\frac{1+X^{2r}}2-X^r.
\]
Every polynomial of degree below $r$ agrees with $W_r$ on at most $r+2$
points of $\mu_{4r}$. Thus this length-$4r$, dimension-$r$ source has
maximum agreement fraction at most
$1/4+1/(2r)$, and capacity gap at most $1/(2r)$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Suppose $P$ agrees on a subset $S$ of size $M\ge r+3$. Write
\[
 F=X^{2r}-2X^r+1-2P=A_S Q,
 \qquad A_S=\prod_{z\in S}(X-z).
\]
The monic quotient $Q$ has degree $k=2r-M\le r-3$.
Its coefficients are determined successively by the coefficients of
$F$ in degrees $2r,\ldots,M$, all above $r$. Those coefficients are
$1,0,\ldots,0$, so monic division gives
$Q\in\mathbb Z[\zeta_{4r}][X]$ and hence
$P\in\mathbb Z[\zeta_{4r},1/2][X]$.
In particular, this conclusion requires no Vandermonde inverse at a
ramified prime.

Reduce at a prime above $r$. The roots in $\mu_{4r}$ collapse to the
four distinct fourth roots of unity, with exactly $r$ original roots
in each reduction bucket. If the selected multiplicities are $n_c$,
then $\overline F$ is divisible by
$\prod_c(X-c)^{n_c}$. Consequently
\[
 \overline F'=-2\overline P'
 \quad\text{is divisible by}\quad
 \prod_{n_c>0}(X-c)^{n_c-1}.
\]
If nonzero, this derivative would have degree at least $M-4\ge r-1$,
whereas $\deg\overline P'\le r-2$. Thus $\overline P'=0$, and
$\deg\overline P<r$ makes $\overline P$ constant.
At the four fourth roots, $\overline W_r$ takes the values
$0,2,i,-i$, in some order. These are distinct in characteristic $r>5$:
a collision of $2$ with $\pm i$ would force characteristic $5$.
Only one bucket can therefore be occupied, contradicting $M>r$.
The agreement-fraction statement follows by division by $4r$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}\label{cor:ordinary-ca-prime-power-obstruction}
If $r=\ell^a$ with $a\ge1$ and prime $\ell>5$, the same
characteristic-zero source has maximum agreement at most
$r+2r/\ell$, and capacity gap at most $1/(2\ell)$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Suppose instead that $M>r+2r/\ell$. The monic-division argument above
again makes $P$ integral at $\ell$. Reduction collapses the selected
roots into four buckets of multiplicities $n_c\le r$ with
$\sum_c n_c=M$. Inductively, after $j$ Frobenius roots, write
\[
 \overline F=F_j^{\ell^j},\qquad
 F_j=X^{2r/\ell^j}-2X^{r/\ell^j}+1-2P_j,
 \qquad \deg P_j<r/\ell^j.
\]
For $0\le j<a$, the derivative is $-2P_j'$ and has root multiplicity
at least $\lceil n_c/\ell^j\rceil-1$ in each occupied bucket.
If it were nonzero, its degree would therefore be at least
\[
 \sum_c\lceil n_c/\ell^j\rceil-4
 \ \ge\ M/\ell^j-4
 \ >\ r/\ell^j-2,
\]
where the last inequality uses
$M-r>2\ell^{a-1}\ge2\ell^j$.
This contradicts $\deg P_j'\le r/\ell^j-2$.
The residue field is perfect, so taking an $\ell$th root gives the
next stage. At $j=a$, $P_a$ is constant and agrees with $W_1$ at
every occupied fourth root. The four values $0,2,i,-i$ are distinct,
so only one bucket is occupied, contradicting $M>r$.
\end{proof}

Thus growing prime orders, and prime-power orders whose prime bases
also grow, cannot supply a fixed positive capacity margin in this
characteristic-zero source, and hence cannot supply a fixed positive
margin above the first-order agreement threshold. This does not exclude
powers of a fixed prime or arbitrary composite orders, nor a separate
positive-characteristic source at another split prime.

More generally, the same induction with $4s$ buckets shows the following
reduction, rather than an exclusion: if $r=\ell^a s$ with $\ell$ an odd prime, $a\ge1$,
$\gcd(\ell,s)=1$, and
$M>r+(4s-2)\ell^{a-1}$, then a polynomial of degree below $s$
agrees with $W_s$ on at least $\lceil M/\ell^a\rceil$ points of
$\mu_{4s}$ in the residue field of characteristic $\ell$.
This field is a prime field when $\ell\equiv1\pmod{4s}$;
in general it is an extension field. The reduction preserves or
increases the normalized capacity gap, but does not rule out the
smaller source. Independent proof audits and the detailed reduction
are recorded in

and
