\section{Quadratic ordinary correlated-agreement failures in large characteristic}
\label{app:ordinary-ca-superlinear}

Corollary~\ref{cor:quadratic-extension-fixed-gap} concerns full agreement-set
MCA at a fixed exact gap. A separate construction excludes even ordinary
correlated agreement, still with quadratically many nearby labels, but
with a gap that is bounded below rather than fixed exactly. The key is
to descend a nearest-polynomial orbit to its own evaluation domain.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:ordinary-ca-superlinear}
For unbounded lengths $n$ there are primes $p$ and Reed--Solomon codes
over $\F_{p^2}$, of exact rate $3/13$, with a received affine line having
at least $\lceil n^2/4096\rceil$ nearby labels at an agreement threshold
$T$, but no ordinary correlated agreement of size $T$.
The capacity gap satisfies $T/n-3/13>3/26$, the characteristic exceeds
the message degree, and the radius is strictly below the
characteristic-based Elias radius for all sufficiently large members.
\end{theorem}

This rules out a linear ordinary-CA bound uniform over gaps bounded below
in the large-characteristic field class. The actual ratio $T/n$ is not
asserted to be constant, nor is $p/n$ bounded above. This is neither a
prime-ambient-field result nor a first-order-regime tightness statement.

\paragraph{An eight-point certificate.}
Let $\zeta$ be a primitive eighth root of unity. No affine polynomial
agrees at three distinct points of $\mu_8$ with
$W_2(Y)=(1+Y^4)/2-Y^2$ in characteristic $p>17$, $p\equiv1\pmod8$.
For completeness, write $v_j=1+\zeta^{4j}-2\zeta^{2j}$. For
$0\le i<j<h<8$, the norm of
\[
 (\zeta^j-\zeta^i)(v_h-v_i)
 -(\zeta^h-\zeta^i)(v_j-v_i)
\]
from $\mathbb Q(\zeta)$ to $\mathbb Q$ has the following complete
distribution, obtained by reduction modulo $Z^4+1$ and resultants:
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrr}
 |\mathrm{Norm}|&64&256&576&1088&3136&4096&6400\\\hline
 \text{number of triples}&12&8&8&8&4&8&8.
\end{array}
\]
All $56$ determinants are nonzero and their norms have prime factors only
$2,3,5,7,17$. Reduction at $p>17$ preserves nonvanishing. Exact verification
and individual determinants are in
\path{research/dickson_fixed_gap/verify_two_orbit.py} and its JSON output.

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:ordinary-ca-superlinear}]
Fix an integer $R\ge40$, and let $B_R$ be the product of odd primes at
most $R$. By the Chinese remainder theorem and Dirichlet's theorem,
there are arbitrarily large primes with
\[
 p\equiv9\pmod{16},\qquad p\equiv-1\pmod{B_R}.
\]
Put $k=(p-1)/4$. Then $v_2(k)=1$, $k\equiv1\pmod3$, and no odd prime
at most $R$ divides $k$. On $\F_p^\times$ use
$W_k(X)=(1+X^{2k})/2-X^k$. Its true maximum agreement $M$ with
degree-$<k$ polynomials is at least $3k/2$ by
Proposition~\ref{prop:full-length-fixed-gap}, and at most $2k$ by degree.
The group $H=\mu_k$ preserves the word and acts on its nearest list
by $P(X)\mapsto P(hX)$.

Take one nearest polynomial and let $r$ be its orbit size. This divides
$k$. If $r\le R$, then $r\in\{1,2\}$, forcing
$P(X)=a+bX^{k/2}$. Passing to $Y=X^{k/2}$, whose eight fibers each have
size $k/2$, the preceding certificate bounds its agreement by $k$,
a contradiction. Thus $r>R$, and $3\nmid r$.

\emph{Descent.} Put $d=k/r$. The stabilizer is $\mu_d$, so
$P(X)=V(X^d)$ with $\deg V<r$. The map $X\mapsto X^d$ sends
$\F_p^\times$ onto $\mu_{4r}$ with fibers of size $d$, and
$W_k(X)=W_r(X^d)$. Consequently $M=dm$, where $m\ge3r/2$ is the
agreement of $V$ with $W_r$ on $\mu_{4r}$. Any closer degree-$<r$
polynomial would lift to a closer degree-$<k$ polynomial, impossible.
The $\mu_r$-orbit of $V$ has exactly $r$ members, all nearest at $m$.
We now have a true nearest list of size $r$ on $4r$ coordinates, in the
same prime field. Its maximum stays $m$ over $\F_{p^2}$: matching $r$
base-field coordinates and values forces base-field coefficients by
interpolation.

\emph{Anchors and rate.} Choose $t\in\{1,2\}$ with $t\equiv r\pmod3$.
At each of $t$ steps, choose an agreement anchor incident to the largest
number of remaining candidates, then divide candidate and word minus
the anchor value by the anchor's linear factor. At step $j=0$ or $1$,
the incidence fraction is $(m-j)/(4r-j)\ge1/3$. At least
$\ell\ge r/9$ quotients remain, with degrees $<K=r-t$ and exactly
$m-t$ old agreements. No polynomial of degree $<K$ has more: restoring
the anchors would contradict the old maximum. Set
\[
 q=(r-10t)/3,\qquad n=4r-t+q=13(r-t)/3,\qquad T=m-t+1.
\]
Here $q\ge r/6>0$, $q\le m-r+1$, $K/n=3/13$, and
\[
 \frac{T-K}{n}=\frac{m-r+1}{n}>\frac3{26}.
\]

\emph{Labels and absence of common agreement.} Put $Q=p^2$.
On the $Q-4r$ points outside the original domain, pairwise root counting
and Cauchy--Schwarz give average distinct-value count at least
\[
 \mu=\frac{\ell(Q-4r)}{Q-4r+(\ell-1)(K-1)}\ge\ell/2,
\]
since $p\ge4r+1$ and $\ell\le r$. Choose the $q$ points with largest
value counts $s_x$, give them direction one, and retain direction zero
on the old domain. Independent uniform translations of the new value
sets have expected union size
\[
 Q\left[1-\prod_x(1-s_x/Q)\right]
 \ge Q[1-(1-\mu/Q)^q]\ge q\ell/4.
\]
The last inequality uses $1-u\le e^{-u}$, $\mu\ge\ell/2$, and
$q\ell/(2Q)\le1/4$. Each counted label adds a new agreement to a
selected quotient, giving at least $T$ agreements. The count is at least
\[
 q\ell/4\ge r^2/216\ge n^2/4056>n^2/4096.
\]
A correlated witness pair $F,G$ of degree $<K$ with $G=0$ has joint
agreements only on the old core, with maximum $m-t=T-1$. If $G\ne0$,
it has at most $K-1$ old zeros and at most $q$ new joint agreements,
again at most $r-t-1+(m-r+1)=m-t=T-1$. Thus ordinary correlated
agreement of size $T$ is absent.

Finally $p\ge4r+1>K-1$ and
$H_p(1-T/n)\le1-T/n+1/\log_2p<1-K/n$ for large $p$.
Taking $R$ unbounded gives unbounded $r$ and $n$.
\end{proof}

The proof does not show that the descended domain is short relative to
$p$. If its orbit sizes could be chosen with $r/p\to0$, the same descent
and the prime-field exact-halving compiler would give superlinear
full-support MCA failures over prime fields at exact rate $1/8$ and gap
$1/16$. This ratio condition remains unproved. Details and independent
small-field checks are in \path{research/ordinary_ca_superlinear/}.


\subsection{Fixing both the rate and the gap}

Allowing larger finite extensions permits an exact positive gap as well.
The following operations preserve the true nearest-codeword boundary;
their field extensions, rather than just fresh coordinates, are essential.

\begin{lemma}[Boundary-preserving padding]\label{lem:boundary-preserving-padding}
Let a word $w$ on $N$ distinct points of a finite field $F$ have maximum
agreement $M\ge K$ with degree-$<K$ polynomials. Keep any selected nearest
list. The following operations preserve that list as nearest witnesses.
\begin{enumerate}
\item If $a\in F$ is outside the domain and $b\notin F$ lies in a finite
extension, appending $(a,b)$ preserves dimension $K$ and maximum $M$.
\item If $a\notin F$, replace every old value by $(x-a)w(x)$, append
$(a,0)$, and increase dimension to $K+1$. The maximum becomes $M+1$;
replace each selected polynomial $P$ by $(X-a)P$.
\end{enumerate}
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
Maximum agreement cannot increase upon extending the coefficient field,
since $K$ matching base-field coordinates and values force coefficients
into $F$. For the first operation, a polynomial with $M+1$ agreements
would match the new point and at least $M\ge K$ old ones. Its coefficients
would belong to $F$, contradicting its new value $b$ at $a\in F$.

For the second operation, the displayed polynomials attain $M+1$.
Suppose a degree-$\le K$ polynomial attained $M+2$. If it matches at $a$,
dividing by $X-a$ contradicts the old maximum. Otherwise take a set $S$
of $M+2$ old matches. Modulo the degree-$\le K$ evaluation space on $S$,
the interpolation condition reads $[xw]-a[w]=0$. This is a linear system
over $F$, so $a\notin F$ forces both classes to vanish. There are
polynomials $A,B$ of degree at most $K$ interpolating $xw,w$ on $S$.
But $A-XB$ has degree at most $K+1$ and at least $M+2\ge K+2$ roots.
Thus $A=XB$, so $\deg B<K$, contradicting the old maximum.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Quadratic ordinary-CA failures at a fixed exact gap]
\label{cor:ordinary-ca-fixed-gap}
For unbounded lengths $n$ there are finite fields of characteristic
$p>n/8-1$ and Reed--Solomon codes of exact rate $1/8$, with a received
line having at least $\lceil n^2/8192\rceil$ nearby labels at agreement
$3n/16$, but no ordinary correlated agreement of that size. The capacity
gap is exactly $1/16$. The radius is strictly below the characteristic-based
Elias radius for all sufficiently large members.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Use the descended source from the proof of
Theorem~\ref{thm:ordinary-ca-superlinear}: length $4r$, dimension $r$,
true maximum $m\in[3r/2,2r]$, and $r$ selected nearest polynomials over
$\F_p$, with $p\ge4r+1$ and $r>40$. Put
\[
 \Delta=m-r+1,\qquad u=13\Delta-3r,\qquad s=2\Delta-r+1.
\]
These are nonnegative integers. First apply the first operation of
Lemma~\ref{lem:boundary-preserving-padding} $u$ times, and then the second
operation $s$ times. One may initially pass to $\F_{p^2}$ to provide
all $u$ fresh first-stage coordinates; each subsequent new value or
common-zero coordinate is chosen outside the entire current field.
A tower of quadratic extensions suffices. The resulting source has
\[
 N'=15\Delta+1,\qquad K'=2\Delta+1,\qquad M'=3\Delta,
\]
and retains $r$ selected nearest polynomials.

An agreement anchor is incident to at least
$\ell\ge M'r/N'\ge r/6$ candidates. Dividing by its linear factor gives
an old core of length $15\Delta$, dimension $2\Delta$, and true maximum
$3\Delta-1$. Append $q=\Delta$ challenge coordinates with direction one,
retaining direction zero on the core. Enlarge the field if necessary to
cardinality $Q\ge2N'r$. The same root-count and random-translation
argument gives at least $q\ell/4\ge\Delta r/24$ nearby labels.
The final parameters are
\[
 n=16\Delta,\qquad K=2\Delta,\qquad T=3\Delta.
\]
A zero explaining direction has common agreement at most $3\Delta-1$
on the core. A nonzero direction has at most $2\Delta-1$ core zeros and
$\Delta$ padding agreements, again at most $3\Delta-1$.
Thus ordinary correlated agreement at threshold $T$ is absent.

Since $\Delta\le r+1$ and $\Delta\ge4$, we have $r\ge3\Delta/4$, giving
\[
 J\ge\Delta r/24\ge\Delta^2/32=n^2/8192.
\]
Also $K-1=2\Delta-1\le2r+1<p$. The exact gap $1/16$ yields the Elias
inequality for large $p$, as before.
\end{proof}

This proves that quadratic dependence on length is sometimes unavoidable
for ordinary correlated-agreement exceptions even at a fixed positive
capacity gap, in the full large-characteristic field class. The extension
degree can grow with $n$: Corollary~\ref{cor:ordinary-ca-fixed-gap} is not
asserted over quadratic extensions or prime fields. Theorem~\ref{thm:ordinary-ca-superlinear}
retains the stronger quadratic-extension restriction with a gap only
bounded below. Neither assertion establishes first-order-regime tightness.

Independent exhaustive checks of both preservation operations, including
a negative control showing failure when the common-zero point is chosen
inside the current field, are in
\path{research/ordinary_ca_superlinear/verify_normalization.py}.

A complete finite census also identifies the nearest list for the full
Dickson word over $\F_{41}$: its maximum agreement is $15$ on $40$
coordinates, and exactly $210$ degree-$<10$ polynomials attain it,
in $21$ multiplicative orbits of size $10$. The four-coset anchor
cover reduces completeness to $52{,}252{,}200$ determining supports;
all witnesses are independently replayed in
\path{research/dickson_nearest_threshold/}. This finite result does
not establish maximum agreement $3/8$ for an unbounded family.

\subsection{A polynomial bound on the extension degree}

\begin{proposition}\label{prop:ordinary-ca-field-degree}
The field in Corollary~\ref{cor:ordinary-ca-fixed-gap} can be taken to be
$\F_{p^e}$ with $e\le4n^2$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
We replace the long tower by two block operations. First, $u$ new received
values can be appended simultaneously in an extension of degree $u+1$:
choose their distinct coordinates in the current field $F$, and choose
values $b_1,\ldots,b_u$ such that $1,b_1,\ldots,b_u$ are linearly
independent over $F$. A degree-$<K$ candidate with a new agreement has
at most $K$ total agreements. Otherwise $K+1$ of its agreements, including
a new one, give a Vandermonde row dependence over $F$ with every coefficient
nonzero, contradicting that independence. Thus a true maximum $M\ge K$
is preserved, as is every selected nearest witness.

For the second block operation, suppose $1\le s\le M-K+1$ and choose
distinct $c_1,\ldots,c_s\in F$. Take $\theta$ of degree $D=K+2s$ over
$F$, and put
\[
 Z(X,Y)=\prod_{i=1}^s(X-Y-c_i).
\]
Append coordinates $\theta+c_i$ with value zero, multiply every old
received value and selected polynomial by $Z(X,\theta)$, and increase
dimension to $K+s$. We claim the maximum is exactly $M+s$.

Suppose a degree-$<K+s$ polynomial $Q$ has $M+s+1$ agreements. At least
$M+1\ge K+s$ are old, so interpolation lifts it to a polynomial
$\widetilde Q(X,Y)$ over $F$ with $X$-degree $<K+s$ and $Y$-degree
at most $s$. At every old agreement $x$,
\[
 \widetilde Q(x,Y)=Z(x,Y)w(x)
\]
as a polynomial identity: the difference has $Y$-degree $\le s<D$ and
vanishes at $\theta$. For each new match $\theta+c_i$, the polynomial
$\widetilde Q(Y+c_i,Y)$ has degree $<K+2s=D$ and vanishes at $\theta$.
It is therefore zero identically, so $X-Y-c_i$ divides $\widetilde Q$.
Divide out all $j$ matching new factors from both $\widetilde Q$ and $Z$.
The resulting $Q_0,Z_0$ have $X$-degrees $<K+h,h$ and $Y$-degrees
$\le h,h$, respectively, where $h=s-j$.

Let $B(X)=(-1)^h[Y^h]Q_0(X,Y)$. On every old agreement, $B(x)=w(x)$.
Each $Y$-coefficient of $Q_0-Z_0B$ has $X$-degree $<K+2h$ and at least
$M+h+1\ge K+2h$ old roots. Consequently $Q_0=Z_0B$, whose $X$-degree
forces $\deg B<K$, contradicting the old maximum. The selected witnesses
attain $M+s$ after multiplication by $Z$, proving the claim.

Apply these operations to the parameters of
Corollary~\ref{cor:ordinary-ca-fixed-gap}. Start over $\F_{p^2}$, perform
the new-value block of size $u=13\Delta-3r$, and then perform two ordinary
single-common-zero operations, costing degree $4$. The remaining number
of common zeros is
\[
 s'=2\Delta-r-1\in[1,\Delta].
\]
The current dimension and maximum are $r+2,m+2$, whose difference plus
one is $\Delta$. The translated-root block therefore applies with
$D=r+2+2s'\le3r+4$. No roots of unity need be adjoined. The total
extension degree satisfies
\[
 e\le8(u+1)D\le8(10r+14)(3r+4)
   <4(8r+16)^2\le4n^2.
\]
The resulting field already has cardinality at least $p^4\ge2N'r$,
so the final label compiler needs no additional extension.
\end{proof}

Complete determining-support checks for the block operations over
$\F_{7^4}$ and $\F_{7^6}$ are recorded in
\path{research/ordinary_ca_superlinear/verify_block_padding.py}.
The bound $e\le4n^2$ is a field-size refinement, not a fixed-degree
extension or prime-field claim.
