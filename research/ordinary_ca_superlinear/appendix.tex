\section{Quadratic ordinary correlated-agreement failures in quadratic extensions}
\label{app:ordinary-ca-superlinear}

Corollary~\ref{cor:quadratic-extension-fixed-gap} concerns full agreement-set
MCA at a fixed exact gap. A separate construction excludes even ordinary
correlated agreement, still with quadratically many nearby labels, but
with a gap that is bounded below rather than fixed exactly. The key is
to descend a nearest-polynomial orbit to its own evaluation domain.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:ordinary-ca-superlinear}
For unbounded lengths $n$ there are primes $p$ and Reed--Solomon codes
over $\F_{p^2}$, of exact rate $3/13$, with a received affine line having
at least $\lceil n^2/4096\rceil$ nearby labels at an agreement threshold
$T$, but no ordinary correlated agreement of size $T$.
The capacity gap satisfies $T/n-3/13>3/26$, the characteristic exceeds
the message degree, and the radius is strictly below the
characteristic-based Elias radius for all sufficiently large members.
\end{theorem}

This rules out a linear ordinary-CA bound uniform over gaps bounded below
in the large-characteristic field class. The actual ratio $T/n$ is not
asserted to be constant, nor is $p/n$ bounded above. This is neither a
prime-ambient-field result nor a first-order-regime tightness statement.

\paragraph{An eight-point certificate.}
Let $\zeta$ be a primitive eighth root of unity. No affine polynomial
agrees at three distinct points of $\mu_8$ with
$W_2(Y)=(1+Y^4)/2-Y^2$ in characteristic $p>17$, $p\equiv1\pmod8$.
For completeness, write $v_j=1+\zeta^{4j}-2\zeta^{2j}$. For
$0\le i<j<h<8$, the norm of
\[
 (\zeta^j-\zeta^i)(v_h-v_i)
 -(\zeta^h-\zeta^i)(v_j-v_i)
\]
from $\mathbb Q(\zeta)$ to $\mathbb Q$ has the following complete
distribution, obtained by reduction modulo $Z^4+1$ and resultants:
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrr}
 |\mathrm{Norm}|&64&256&576&1088&3136&4096&6400\\\hline
 \text{number of triples}&12&8&8&8&4&8&8.
\end{array}
\]
All $56$ determinants are nonzero and their norms have prime factors only
$2,3,5,7,17$. Reduction at $p>17$ preserves nonvanishing. Exact verification
and individual determinants are in
\path{research/dickson_fixed_gap/verify_two_orbit.py} and its JSON output.

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:ordinary-ca-superlinear}]
Fix an integer $R\ge40$, and let $B_R$ be the product of odd primes at
most $R$. By the Chinese remainder theorem and Dirichlet's theorem,
there are arbitrarily large primes with
\[
 p\equiv9\pmod{16},\qquad p\equiv-1\pmod{B_R}.
\]
Put $k=(p-1)/4$. Then $v_2(k)=1$, $k\equiv1\pmod3$, and no odd prime
at most $R$ divides $k$. On $\F_p^\times$ use
$W_k(X)=(1+X^{2k})/2-X^k$. Its true maximum agreement $M$ with
degree-$<k$ polynomials is at least $3k/2$ by
Proposition~\ref{prop:full-length-fixed-gap}, and at most $2k$ by degree.
The group $H=\mu_k$ preserves the word and acts on its nearest list
by $P(X)\mapsto P(hX)$.

Take one nearest polynomial and let $r$ be its orbit size. This divides
$k$. If $r\le R$, then $r\in\{1,2\}$, forcing
$P(X)=a+bX^{k/2}$. Passing to $Y=X^{k/2}$, whose eight fibers each have
size $k/2$, the preceding certificate bounds its agreement by $k$,
a contradiction. Thus $r>R$, and $3\nmid r$.

\emph{Descent.} Put $d=k/r$. The stabilizer is $\mu_d$, so
$P(X)=V(X^d)$ with $\deg V<r$. The map $X\mapsto X^d$ sends
$\F_p^\times$ onto $\mu_{4r}$ with fibers of size $d$, and
$W_k(X)=W_r(X^d)$. Consequently $M=dm$, where $m\ge3r/2$ is the
agreement of $V$ with $W_r$ on $\mu_{4r}$. Any closer degree-$<r$
polynomial would lift to a closer degree-$<k$ polynomial, impossible.
The $\mu_r$-orbit of $V$ has exactly $r$ members, all nearest at $m$.
We now have a true nearest list of size $r$ on $4r$ coordinates, in the
same prime field. Its maximum stays $m$ over $\F_{p^2}$: matching $r$
base-field coordinates and values forces base-field coefficients by
interpolation.

\emph{Anchors and rate.} Choose $t\in\{1,2\}$ with $t\equiv r\pmod3$.
At each of $t$ steps, choose an agreement anchor incident to the largest
number of remaining candidates, then divide candidate and word minus
the anchor value by the anchor's linear factor. At step $j=0$ or $1$,
the incidence fraction is $(m-j)/(4r-j)\ge1/3$. At least
$\ell\ge r/9$ quotients remain, with degrees $<K=r-t$ and exactly
$m-t$ old agreements. No polynomial of degree $<K$ has more: restoring
the anchors would contradict the old maximum. Set
\[
 q=(r-10t)/3,\qquad n=4r-t+q=13(r-t)/3,\qquad T=m-t+1.
\]
Here $q\ge r/6>0$, $q\le m-r+1$, $K/n=3/13$, and
\[
 \frac{T-K}{n}=\frac{m-r+1}{n}>\frac3{26}.
\]

\emph{Labels and absence of common agreement.} Put $Q=p^2$.
On the $Q-4r$ points outside the original domain, pairwise root counting
and Cauchy--Schwarz give average distinct-value count at least
\[
 \mu=\frac{\ell(Q-4r)}{Q-4r+(\ell-1)(K-1)}\ge\ell/2,
\]
since $p\ge4r+1$ and $\ell\le r$. Choose the $q$ points with largest
value counts $s_x$, give them direction one, and retain direction zero
on the old domain. Independent uniform translations of the new value
sets have expected union size
\[
 Q\left[1-\prod_x(1-s_x/Q)\right]
 \ge Q[1-(1-\mu/Q)^q]\ge q\ell/4.
\]
The last inequality uses $1-u\le e^{-u}$, $\mu\ge\ell/2$, and
$q\ell/(2Q)\le1/4$. Each counted label adds a new agreement to a
selected quotient, giving at least $T$ agreements. The count is at least
\[
 q\ell/4\ge r^2/216\ge n^2/4056>n^2/4096.
\]
A correlated witness pair $F,G$ of degree $<K$ with $G=0$ has joint
agreements only on the old core, with maximum $m-t=T-1$. If $G\ne0$,
it has at most $K-1$ old zeros and at most $q$ new joint agreements,
again at most $r-t-1+(m-r+1)=m-t=T-1$. Thus ordinary correlated
agreement of size $T$ is absent.

Finally $p\ge4r+1>K-1$ and
$H_p(1-T/n)\le1-T/n+1/\log_2p<1-K/n$ for large $p$.
Taking $R$ unbounded gives unbounded $r$ and $n$.
\end{proof}

The proof does not show that the descended domain is short relative to
$p$. If its orbit sizes could be chosen with $r/p\to0$, the same descent
and the prime-field exact-halving compiler would give superlinear
full-support MCA failures over prime fields at exact rate $1/8$ and gap
$1/16$. This ratio condition remains unproved. Details and independent
small-field checks are in \path{research/ordinary_ca_superlinear/}.
