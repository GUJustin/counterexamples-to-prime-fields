# Partial packets and arbitrary common denominators: a new scoped obstruction

September 18, 2026. Pure polynomial identities on the prescribed KoalaBear
domain. No improved agreement/count certificate or new score is obtained.

This extends the earlier fixed-head factor gate in two ways: the partial
support rigidity below **does not assume shared packet coefficients**, and
the final coefficient-dimension bound permits **every common denominator**
that is nonzero on the domain, of arbitrary degree. The bound is on the
ambient coefficient space; it is not an upper bound on a special root-subset
fiber's cardinality.

Set

```
p = 2130706433, E = F_(p^6), D = mu_262144 in F_p,
B = 512, J = 131072, A = 139782.
```

The incumbent has agreement 139775, 272 whole B-packets, and a fixed
511-point core. Its fourteen heads and root product impose fifteen
independent affine conditions on a formal monic packet polynomial.

## 1. A common top band follows from more than p labels

Suppose a bank with more than p distinct labels satisfies polynomial
identities

```
F0 + gamma F1 - T P_gamma = lambda_gamma A_gamma,
deg P_gamma < J, lambda_gamma != 0,
```

where every A_gamma is a monic degree-A polynomial over F_p. In the
application A_gamma is the squarefree locator of A domain points. Assume
`m = A - (deg T + J - 1) > 0`.

All A_gamma have the same coefficients at offsets `0,...,m-1` from the
top, including the leading coefficient. Indeed the leading coefficient
gives `lambda_gamma = a + b gamma`. At each other such offset, the
normalized coefficient has the form

```
(a_j + b_j gamma)/(a + b gamma) in F_p.
```

A nonconstant fractional linear function is injective where its
denominator is nonzero, so it allows at most p labels. More than p labels
force every displayed coefficient to be constant. In particular,

```
A_gamma / A_eta = 1 + O(X^(-m)) at infinity.
```

For `deg T <= 1031`, we have `m >= 7680`: **7679 nonleading
coefficients** are fixed, without prescribing any heads in advance.

## 2. Few exchanged whole packets cannot create variable partial patterns

Suppose

```
A_gamma(X) = R0(X) S_gamma(X) V_gamma(X^B),
```

with R0 fixed, and all factors monic and supported on distinct domain
roots. Put `e_gamma = deg S_gamma`; the degrees of V_gamma may differ.
If `e_gamma + e_eta < m`, then

```
S_gamma / S_eta in F_p(X^B).
```

To prove this, let zeta have order B. The packet-polynomial ratio is
unchanged by `X -> zeta X`. The common top band therefore bounds the
degree of

```
S_gamma(zeta X) S_eta(X) - S_gamma(X) S_eta(zeta X)
```

by `e_gamma + e_eta - m < 0`. It vanishes. Invariance under the cyclic
group generated by zeta gives the stated fixed field.

At each B-packet, valuations of S_gamma/S_eta are consequently constant
throughout that packet. Because the factors are squarefree, two supports
can differ there only by a whole packet. After factoring all whole
B-packets out of every S_gamma, the remaining partial locator is identical
throughout the bank. This last qualification is essential: the lemma does
not freeze full packets that were unnecessarily left inside S_gamma.

Two exact applications at `deg T <= 1031` are:

* With no prescribed core, any degree-139782 support with at least 266
  whole packets has partial degree at most
  `139782 - 266*512 = 3590`; twice this is 7180 < 7680. Its partial
  occupancy pattern is fixed. This covers replacing up to six of the
  incumbent's 272 whole packets, even if the original core is changed.
* Keeping the original 511-point core, a support with at least 265 other
  whole packets has additional factor degree at most
  `139782 - 511 - 265*512 = 3591`; twice this is 7182 < 7680. This
  covers replacing up to seven whole packets. Again, whole packets are
  removed from that factor before stating that its partial pattern is fixed.

The preceding fixed-head gate required the original fourteen heads.
These conclusions follow from the affine identity and the label count
alone. Larger partial factors, or a denominator large enough to shorten
the common band, are outside this particular rigidity conclusion.

## 3. Any common denominator leaves an extra coefficient condition

Here the partial locator R is fixed, monic, of degree c, with all its
roots in D. Let packet polynomials V be monic of degree h, with
`hB+c=A`. No shared-head assumption is made, and deg T is now arbitrary.
Assume only that T is nonzero at every domain point.

Let tau(Y) generate the ideal

```
{q(Y) in E[Y] : T(X) divides q(X^B)}.
```

The ideal is nonzero (multiplication by X^B in E[X]/(T) has a minimal
polynomial). Put `d=deg T`, `a=deg tau`; since `T | tau(X^B)`,
`a >= ceil(d/B)`. As gcd(R,T)=1, the kernel of the map

```
q(Y) -> R(X) q(X^B) modulo {T P : deg P < J}
```

on polynomials of degree at most h consists precisely of multiples of
tau whose degree is at most

```
min(h, floor((J-1+d-c)/B)).
```

Its E-dimension kappa is therefore at most

```
max(0, floor((J-1-c)/B) + 1).                 (1)
```

Indeed `floor(u+d/B)-ceil(d/B) <= floor(u)`.

Allow arbitrary nonzero error normalizations lambda_gamma. In the same
quotient, every V_gamma maps into the E-span of the classes of F0 and
F1, a space of dimension at most two. Its preimage has dimension at most
`kappa+2`. Intersecting with the monic hyperplane lowers this to at most
`kappa+1`: the leading-coefficient functional is nonzero on a space that
contains a monic polynomial.

At A=139782 and `h >= 17` (in particular throughout the partial-packet
regime above), formula (1) gives `kappa <= h-17`, hence the monic packet
coefficient vectors occupy an E-affine space of dimension at most
`h-16`. Their intersection with F_p^h is an F_p-affine space of dimension
at most h-16 as well. To see the latter, F_p-linearly independent vectors
remain E-linearly independent, by a nonzero minor over F_p.

Thus **at least sixteen independent base-field affine coefficient
conditions are unavoidable** in this fixed-partial-locator model, for
any root-free common denominator. At the incumbent A=139775, the bound
is fifteen, and its fourteen heads plus product attain that dimension.

More generally, when the bound is positive, the required codimension is
at least

```
min(h-1, ceil((A-J+1)/B) - 2).
```

This does not assert that the sixteenth constraint costs a factor p on
the actual combinatorial bank. A special dependence or concentration of
root-subset coefficients could make its image small. Such a new counting
theorem is precisely what remains necessary. Dimension alone does not
exclude a sufficiently large exceptional fiber.

## 4. An explicit denominator exchange attains, but does not beat, the bound

Take a fixed 518-point locator R occupying two partial packets, and
272 whole packets avoiding both. Fix fourteen top coefficients of V,
its constant coefficient, and its coefficient of Y. For a reference V0
write Delta=V0-V, choose a pole alpha outside the packet labels and zero,
and set

```
gamma = Delta(alpha)/alpha^2,
Q(Y) = [Delta(Y)-gamma Y^2]/[Y^2(Y-alpha)],
T(X) = X^519 (X^512-alpha),
P(X) = R(X) X^505 Q(X^512).
```

The two fixed low coefficients give divisibility by Y^2; the definition
of gamma gives the remaining factor. The fourteen heads give
`deg Delta <= 257`, hence `deg Q <= 254`. Consequently

```
deg T = 1031,
deg P <= 518+505+254*512 = 131071,
F0 = R V0(X^512), F1 = -R X^1024,
F0 + gamma F1 - T P = R V(X^512).
```

Dividing by the root-free T gives the desired 139782 root agreements.
This identity trades one additional coarse head for one additional low
coefficient; it does not remove the sixteenth condition.

The elementary pigeonhole guarantee here is only

```
ceil(C(510,272)/(512*p^15)) = 880067499.
```

For comparison, 273 whole packets and a six-point core, with the ordinary
two-pole identity, give

```
ceil(C(511,273)/(512*p^15)) = 1647305831,
```

still short of 274980728111395088 by a factor over 166 million. These are
guaranteed bank sizes, not upper bounds on possible special fibers.

The next useful gate is a proved root-support dependence that makes a
sixteenth coefficient condition cheap, or a support family outside the
short-partial-factor regime. The existing fixed-head append-factor
construction, arbitrary root-free common denominators with a fixed
partial pattern, and a few whole-to-partial packet exchanges supply no
such dependence. No computational search or external compute is justified
by this calculation alone.

The model uses polynomial identities, a common denominator, and error
locators supported entirely on the base-field domain. It does not cover
support-dependent denominators, uncancelled poles on the domain, extra
extension-field error roots, or identities whose essential degree reduction
comes from working modulo X^262144-1.

`verify_partial_packet_denominator.py` records the exact thresholds,
dimension arithmetic, and bank comparisons. The algebraic lemmas above,
rather than an enumeration, are the substantive certificate.
