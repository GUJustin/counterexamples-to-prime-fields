The relative separation can also tend to one within a single family.
The maximum distance from a dimension-$K$ Reed--Solomon code is
$n-K$ coordinates: interpolation always gives agreement on $K$
positions, and a monic degree-$K$ word attains that distance. Thus
the next result's exceptional point is only one coordinate short of
being maximally far.

\begin{corollary}[Almost the entire capacity gap]\label{pd:full-gap}
Fix rational $\rho\in(0,1)$ and write $b=\log_2p$. Let
$r=r(p)\to\infty$ satisfy $r\log b=o(b)$.
Over every sufficiently large prime field, there are codes of exact
rate $\rho$, length $n\sim2rb/H_2(\rho)$, and gap
$\eta\sim H_2(\rho)/b$ with a received line whose every nonzero
parameter is nearby, while parameter zero has distance
\[
 1-\rho-1/n
 =1-\rho-\eta+(1-o(1))\eta.
\]
The radius is strictly below Elias, there is no correlated agreement,
and $n2^{H_2(\rho)/\eta}=o(p)$. The same efficient sampling guarantee
holds. In particular, $r=\lfloor\log_2b\rfloor$ gives
$n=\Theta_\rho(b\log b)$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Let $H=H_2(\rho)$, $J=-\log_2(1-\rho)>0$, and $t=1/(4J)$. Choose
\[
 n=\frac{(2r+1/2)b}{H}+O(1),\qquad
 m=\frac{n-tb}{2}+O(r),\qquad D=\frac{\rho n}{2}+O(1),
\]
with the same parity and block-divisibility adjustments. Then
$q\sim tb/(2r)\to\infty$. Since $H-\rho H_2'(\rho)=J$, Taylor
expansion and the binomial entropy estimate give
\[
 \log_2 L
 =\frac{nH}{2}-\frac{tbJ}{2}
   +O_\rho(b/r+r+\log b)
 =rb+b/8+o(b).
\]
Thus $L\ge p^{r+1/9}$ eventually. Moreover
$(4r+2)^{2r+2}=p^{o(1)}$ and $m^2=p^{o(1)}$, so
$\delta=p^{-\Omega(1)}$. Taking $\gamma=p^{-1/20}$ makes the first
failure term tend to zero polynomially in $p$, and the second is at
most $p^{1-q/20}$.

The separation fraction is $2r/(2r+1)\to1$. Also
$\eta b-H=\Theta_\rho(1/r)$, which dominates the
$O_\rho(1/b)$ change from $H_2(\rho)$ to $H_2(\rho+\eta)$;
this proves strict Elias. Finally
\[
 \log_2\left(\frac{n2^{H/\eta}}p\right)
 =\log_2n-\frac{b}{4r+2}+O_\rho(1/r)\longrightarrow-\infty.
\]
\end{proof}
