\subsubsection{Deterministic banks over the 31-bit Mersenne prime}
\label{sec:paired-m31}

The preceding small-bit full-coverage certificates use averaging. At
$p=2^{31}-1$, direct enumeration instead certifies the following large
banks for concrete domains. These counts do not depend on a sampling
success event.

\begin{proposition}[Explicit M31 banks, verified by enumeration]
\label{pd:m31-exact}
Over $\F_{2^{31}-1}$ there are the following Reed--Solomon codes and
received lines. Put $\rho=K/n$, $\eta=3/n$, and
$B=n2^{H_2(\rho)/\eta}$.
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}rrrrl@{}}
\toprule
$n$ & $K$ & Candidate supports & Certified nearby parameters & Comparison\\
\midrule
58&27&40,116,600&39,763,294&$>B$\\
62&31&145,422,675&140,916,078&$>B$\\
64&31&300,540,195&281,212,602&$>B$\\
68&33&1,166,803,110&897,817,238&$>2B$\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
Parameter zero has exact distance $(n-K-1)/n$, and every certified
parameter has exact distance $(n-K-3)/n$. The direction changes only
two coordinates. All four radii are strictly below Elias and have no
correlated agreement. Thus all four violate the $c_1=c_2=1$ numerical
prescription; the last also violates $c_1=2,c_2=1$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}[Construction and computational verification]
Let $m=n/2-1$ and $D=(K+1)/2$. Use the first $m$ entries of the following
list as core representatives, with $\pm1$ as the padding pair:
\[
\begin{gathered}
848979899,\ 738121439,\ 699266884,\ 128913239,\ 806814741,\ 629503248,\\
179005809,\ 134797481,\ 207154190,\ 1023774369,\ 339671428,\ 287462841,\\
393054946,\ 39961481,\ 1026875673,\ 850367968,\ 913861226,\ 264277071,\\
48753053,\ 60664611,\ 1054291373,\ 882987304,\ 134769212,\ 35446086,\\
175650241,\ 263723351,\ 718238076,\ 6429846,\ 428509650,\ 268381772,\\
130012564,\ 236350235,\ 900863422.
\end{gathered}
\]
The domain consists of both signs of these representatives and of $1$.
Set $w(X)=\prod_{i=1}^D(X^2-a_i^2)$, let $f$ be its evaluation word,
and let $g$ equal one on $\pm1$ and zero elsewhere. A $D$-subset $I$
supplies the codeword $w-H_I$ at parameter
\[
 z_I=-\prod_{i\in I}(1-a_i^2)\ne0.
\]
It has $K+1$ core and two padding agreements. The root bound at zero
and the two-coordinate distance inequality prove both exact distances.

Two independent programs in \path{research/paired_domain_warp/m31_exact/}
enumerate the image of these subset products. One joins product lists
from two halves of the core and uses Mersenne reduction; the other
recursively enumerates complementary supports, using inverse factors
and ordinary modular reduction. Their bitmaps give the same four
counts in the table. The verifier also recovers explicit supports and
codeword coefficients for selected parameters. The counts are exact
for this candidate family and lower-bound the total number of nearby
parameters on the line.

The comparison with $c_1B$ is checked by the integer inequality
\[
 J^3K^K(n-K)^{n-K}>c_1^3n^{n+3},
\]
where $J$ is the certified count. In the half-rate row,
$B=62\cdot2^{62/3}<103199661$. Finally
$\eta\log_2p>90/n>1$ proves strict Elias in every row.
The code's prescribed domain is the displayed paired set; a power-of-two
length in the third row does not make it an FFT-domain example.
\end{proof}


\paragraph{Exact local lists in the half-rate row.}
For $n=62,K=31$, the signed-relation condition of
Lemma~\ref{pd:unique-criterion} is also checked exhaustively. Splitting
its $30$ variables into two groups gives $3^{15}=14348907$ assignments
per group; the only matching pair is the empty assignment. Two
independent implementations agree. The lemma therefore excludes every
nearby codeword outside the enumerated locator family. Two further
independent counts give its complete list-size distribution:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{@{}rrrrrrr@{}}
\toprule
List size&0&1&2&3&4&5\\
\midrule
Parameters&2,006,567,569&136,494,714&4,337,028&83,442&891&3\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
In particular, even the $136494714$ uniquely nearby parameters exceed
$B<103199661$. Every point on this line has at most five nearby
codewords at the tested radius. This is an exact statement about the
displayed line, not a global list-size upper bound for the code.
