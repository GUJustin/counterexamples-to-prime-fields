\paragraph{Reproducible finite examples.}
For $L=25$, the parameters are $n=1201$, $K=3$, $A=48$, and $T=49$.
Over $\mathbb F_{2000003}$, an exact certificate realizes all $15026$
singleton exceptions in Theorem~\ref{thm:prime-quadratic-intersection-line}.
A separate checker verifies the domain, labels, witnesses, and structural
exclusion of every other quadratic; the certificate and replay are in
\path{research/binary_characteristic_transfer/prime_L25/}.
A collision-permitting domain over $\mathbb F_{20011}$ has $12630$ finite
bad labels. Including the additional projective label, there are exactly
$12631$ bad challenges and exactly $10798$ singleton threshold lists
($53.96\%$ of the entire field). These counts use exact modular arithmetic
and the same degree-based completeness argument, rather than an exhaustive
scan of all $p^3$ quadratics. The domain and independent replay are in
\path{research/binary_characteristic_transfer/pooled_prime_labels/}.

A larger verified domain has $L=363$, $n=262813$, $p=47700761$,
and threshold $T=725$. It has exactly $31301643$ bad challenges,
including exactly $19622801$ singleton threshold lists ($41.14\%$
of the field). The winning domain, direct polynomial replay, and bounded
CPU-search receipt are archived in the same directory.
