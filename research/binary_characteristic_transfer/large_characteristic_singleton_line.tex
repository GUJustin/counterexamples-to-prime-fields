% Baseline: the existing fixed-characteristic locator compiler.
% New refinement: exhaustive singleton classification below Johnson.
\begin{proposition}[A low-rate large-characteristic line with singleton nearest lists]
\label{prop:low-rate-large-characteristic-singletons}
Let $p\ge5$ be prime, let $\mathbb B=\mathbb F_{p^5}$, and let
$\mathbb F=\mathbb F_{p^{15}}$. Choose $\theta\in\mathbb F\setminus\mathbb B$; then
$1,\theta,\theta^2$ are linearly independent over $\mathbb B$.
On the evaluation domain $\mathbb B$, put
\[
 N=p^5,\qquad K=p,\qquad
 f=X^{p^4-1}+\theta X^{p^3-1}+\theta^2X^{p^2-1},
 \qquad g=X^{p^2-1}.
\]
For degree-$<K$ witnesses, both individual source agreements and their
common agreement equal $C=p^2-1$.
Exactly
\[
 M=\genfrac{[}{]}{0pt}{}{5}{2}_p
  =p^6+p^5+2p^4+2p^3+2p^2+p+1
\]
nonzero challenges $z\in\mathbb F$ have nearest agreement $A=p^3-1$.
Exactly $M$ further nonzero challenges have nearest agreement $p^2$;
all other challenges have nearest agreement $C=p^2-1$.
At every challenge in either of the first two classes, the list at
threshold $p^2$ consists of exactly one polynomial.
Consequently, at every integer threshold $p^2<T\le A$, the qualifying
labels are exactly the first $M$ labels and each qualifying list is a singleton.

For $p\ge53$, exactly these $M>N^{6/5}$ challenges are bad at the lower advertised threshold
$A_0=p^3-p^2$. This threshold is below $\sqrt{N(K-1)}$ and above the
first-order curve $Na_1(K/N)$. An explicit first-order certificate uses
\[
 m=4,\qquad B_\partial=2,\qquad B_{\rm jet}=4p^2,
 \qquad B_Z=192p^2.
\]
In particular its counting characteristic guard
$p>\max\{K-1,B_\partial\}$ holds. The finite certificate and squarefree counting formulas
of~\cite[Proposition~5.10 and Lemma~5.6]{dkt2026} give the uniform
upper bound $68N$ for the list at threshold $A_0$ of every received word
on this evaluation set. For every affine received line, they give at most
$300000N^2$ exceptional challenges for full-agreement-set MCA recovery
at $A_0$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For every three-dimensional $\mathbb F_p$-subspace $W\subseteq\mathbb B$,
write its monic locator as
\[
 L_W=X^{p^3}+aX^{p^2}+cX^p+vX.
\]
Its linear coefficient $v$ is nonzero. Define
\[
 z_W=c^p-a^{p+1}+\theta a-\theta^2,
 \qquad
 h_W=-\bigl((\theta-a^p)c+v^p\bigr)X^{p-1}
        -(\theta-a^p)v.
\]
The locator compiler identity is
\[
 X(f+z_Wg-h_W)=L_W^p+(\theta-a^p)L_W.
\]
On $\mathbb B$ the second factor in
$L_W(L_W^{p-1}+\theta-a^p)$ never vanishes. Since $h_W(0)\ne0$,
the agreement set is exactly $W\setminus\{0\}$.
The coordinates of $z_W$ in $1,\theta,\theta^2$ determine $a,c$.
Two such locators with the same $a,c$ differ only by a multiple of $X$;
their subspaces intersect in a nonzero vector, so they coincide.
Thus the displayed $M$ labels are distinct and nonzero.

We prove exhaustiveness already at threshold $p^2$, including arbitrary
witness coefficients in $\mathbb F$. Projecting an agreement equation
onto its $\theta^2$ coordinate gives a polynomial of degree at most
$p^2-1$ with at least $p^2$ roots. Hence the $\theta^2$ coordinates of
$z,h$ are respectively $-1,0$. Write
$z=b+\theta a-\theta^2$ and $h=h_0+\theta h_1$, with coefficients
in $\mathbb B$, and set
\[
 L=X^{p^3}+aX^{p^2}-Xh_1,\qquad
 T=X^{p^4}+bX^{p^2}-Xh_0.
\]
The polynomial
\[
 \frac{T-L^p+a^pL}{X}
 =(b+a^{p+1})X^{p^2-1}+X^{p-1}h_1^p-h_0-a^ph_1
\]
has degree at most $p^2-1$ and vanishes at every agreement coordinate.
At a matched zero this follows from $h_0(0)=h_1(0)=0$, so division by
$X$ loses no required root. It is identically zero. The distinct
exponents $p-1+pj$ force $h_1$ to have only degrees $0,p-1$.
Coefficient comparison gives precisely
\[
 L=X^{p^3}+aX^{p^2}+cX^p+vX,\qquad
 z=c^p-a^{p+1}+\theta a-\theta^2,
\]
and the displayed formula for $h_W$, whether or not $L$ is a
three-space locator.

If $v\ne0$, zero is not matched and the agreement count is
$|\ker_{\mathbb B}L|-1$. At least $p^2$ agreements therefore forces
$L$ to be a three-space locator, giving the first class.
If $v=0$, then $L=J^p$, where
\[
 J=X^{p^2}+a^{1/p}X^p+c^{1/p}X.
\]
Zero is now matched, and at least $p^2$ agreements forces $J$ to be
the locator of a two-dimensional subspace $U$. Each such $U$ supplies
a distinct secondary label, with exactly $p^2$ agreements; there are
$\genfrac{[}{]}{0pt}{}{5}{2}_p=M$ of them.

The two label classes are disjoint. Otherwise their identical $a,c$
would give $L_W=J_U^p+vX$ with $v\ne0$.
Their kernels intersect trivially, so $\mathbb B=U\oplus W$.
The $\mathbb F_p$-linear map $x\mapsto-J_U(x)^p/v$ is the projection
onto $W$ along $U$, of operator trace $3$. But its linearized polynomial
has no $X$ term. For a map $\sum_{i=0}^4 a_i x^{p^i}$ on $\mathbb B$,
the operator trace is $\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathbb B/\mathbb F_p}(a_0)$:
after extending scalars, multiplication is diagonal in the five embedding
coordinates, while every nonidentity Frobenius power permutes these
coordinates without a fixed point. Thus this projection has trace zero,
a contradiction since $p\ne3$.
The coefficient comparison and injectivity show that each label in
either class has a singleton list at threshold $p^2$.

The source upper bounds follow by projecting $f$ onto its
$\theta^2$ coordinate and by using the degree of $g$: either source
has at most $p^2-1$ agreements with a strict witness. For the matching
common lower bound, choose any two-dimensional subspace $U$.
Remainders of the additive heads modulo $L_U$ have degree at most
$p$; division by $X$ gives simultaneous degree-$<p$ witnesses on
$U\setminus\{0\}$. Hence both individual agreements and common
agreement equal $C$. The same simultaneous witnesses give every word
on the line at least $C$ agreements. Exhaustiveness above therefore
gives nearest agreement exactly $C$ at all remaining challenges,
completing the three-level classification.

Assume now $p\ge53$. For completeness, the stated finite first-order comparison involves
no characteristic-dependent hidden constant. Write $d=p-1$ and
$B=4p^2$. The derivative-weighted support
\[
 X^xY_0^uY_1^v,\qquad v\le2,\quad u+v\le B,
 \quad x+du+(d-1)v<4A_0
\]
has
\[
 G=24p^5-24p^4-6p^3+18p^2+p-3>\tfrac{47}{2}N
\]
coefficients. Its local-rank bound is $23$, from Taylor layers
$3,6,8,6$. With $H=48B$, the graded challenge test follows from
\[
 (H-B+1)G>(47B+1)\tfrac{47}{2}N>(H+1)23N.
\]
The finite squarefree counting formulas
of~\cite[Eqs.~(38), (54), and (55)]{dkt2026} apply with derivative cap two,
even though $B>p$. To record a reproducible coarse budget, their
parameters satisfy
\[
 \lambda<2p^2,\quad F_{\rm reg}\le28p^3,\quad
 S<12p^2,\quad H_s=576p^2,\quad J_1\le30776p^6.
\]
Choose both incidence thresholds $\lfloor A_0/2\rfloor$.
The two regular terms are at most $184656p^{10}$ and $112p^{10}$;
the ordinary tail is at most $82956p^7+15588p^4$.
These give the asserted bounds.
Finally $A_0^2<N(K-1)$, while the low-rate curve estimate
\[
 a_1(p^{-4})\le\frac{p^{-2}}{\sqrt2}
     +\frac{p^{-3}}{2^{3/4}\sqrt3}
     <p^{-2}(1-1/p)
\]
holds for $p\ge53$.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Scope.}
The baseline population is an explicit specialization of the existing
fixed-characteristic locator compiler
of~\cite{binary-counterexamples}; the refinement is its exhaustive
singleton-list classification. The ambient field is an extension field,
the rate tends to zero, and the absolute first-order margin tends to zero.
Singleton classification holds throughout $p^2<T\le A$, including
the below-Johnson advertised threshold $A_0$. This is a statement about
the displayed received line, not a bound on lists of arbitrary received words.
The ratio $A_0/(Na_1(K/N))$ tends to $\sqrt2$, but this does not establish
fixed-rate or fixed-margin tightness.
