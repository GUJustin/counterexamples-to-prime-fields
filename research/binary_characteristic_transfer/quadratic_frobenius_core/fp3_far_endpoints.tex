\subsection{One-fiber puncturing: an exact spectrum with far inputs}
\label{sec:fp3-far-endpoints}

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:fp3-far-endpoints}
For every prime $p\ge4099$, there is a dimension-three Reed--Solomon
code over $E=\mathbb F_{p^3}$, of length
\[
 2p^2-2p-1\le n\le2p^2-p-1,
\]
and a received affine line with the following exact list-size spectrum
at agreement threshold
\[
 T=2p-\lceil4\sqrt p\rceil-4:
\]
exactly $p^3-2p$ parameters have singleton lists, $p$ parameters have
lists of size $p$, and $p$ parameters have empty lists. The empty-list
parameters form an affine $\mathbb F_p$-line, and their received words
all have individual agreement at most $p+2\sqrt p$.
Choose any two distinct such words as endpoints. Their ordinary common
agreement is at most $p+2\sqrt p$, and a uniform $E$-valued affine
mixture is nearby with probability $1-p^{-2}$ and has a singleton list
with probability $1-2p^{-2}$. Moreover
\[
 n a_1(3/n)<T<\sqrt{2n},\qquad
 T-(p+2\sqrt p)\ge p-6\sqrt p-5.
\]
Thus the source separation is $\Theta(\sqrt n)$ and the characteristic
is $\Theta(\sqrt n)$. There are $p$ distinct collinear far inputs
available; these are not affinely independent. The rate and normalized
source separation tend to zero.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Start with Theorem~\ref{thm:fp3-common-agreement}, at length
$N=2p^2-p-1$ and threshold $A=2p-\lceil4\sqrt p\rceil-2$.
Retain its notation $\Lambda_0=(\delta/u_0)\mathbb F_p$.
Its proof classifies all competitors: outside $\Lambda_0$ there is
exactly one doubly canonical quadratic, and every other quadratic has
at most $\max(p+2\sqrt p,16)=p+2\sqrt p$ matches. At each parameter
in $\Lambda_0$, exactly $p$ canonical quadratics meet threshold $A$,
and all other quadratics have at most the same smaller bound.

Put $u_1=\eta u_0\in W$. Then
\[
 I_{u_1}=\eta^{-1}\Lambda_0,\qquad
 \eta I_{u_1}=\Lambda_0,\qquad I_{u_1}\ne\Lambda_0.
\]
The nonzero core fibers of $L_{u_1}$ partition $D_0$ minus its zero
fiber. Their total physical size is $p^2-p$, over $p-1$ possible
nonzero values. Choose $b_*\in I_{u_1}^*$ whose core fiber $C_*$ has
size at most $p$, and delete $C_*$ from the domain. This gives
the asserted length bounds. Put
\[
 Q_*=a_{u_1}X^2+b_*,\qquad \Lambda_*=b_*+\Lambda_0.
\]
The two parameter lines $\Lambda_*,\Lambda_0$ are disjoint.
For every $\lambda\in\Lambda_*$, the unique doubly canonical witness
is $Q_*$, because $\lambda=b_*-\eta v$ for some $v\in I_{u_1}$.
Deleting $C_*$ removes its entire core agreement; its remaining fresh
fiber has at most $p+2\sqrt p$ matches. Every other quadratic was
already below that bound, so these $p$ parameters are far at threshold
$T=A-2>p+2\sqrt p$.

For any generic parameter outside $\Lambda_*$, let $Q_\lambda$ be its
unique high witness. If its leading coefficient differs from
$a_{u_1}$, a common core match with $Q_*$ is a root of the nonzero
quadratic $Q_\lambda-Q_*$, so at most two matches are removed.
If its leading coefficient is $a_{u_1}$, its constant term differs
from $b_*$; the corresponding core fibers are disjoint, and no matches
are removed. Hence $Q_\lambda$ retains at least $A-2=T$ matches.
All its competitors remain below $T$. There are exactly $p^3-2p$
such generic parameters, and their lists are singleton.

At $\lambda\in\Lambda_0$, consider the $p$ qualifying quadratics
from the unpunctured classification. The one of direction $u_1$ has
constant term zero, because $I_{u_1}\cap\Lambda_0=\{0\}$; its core
fiber is disjoint from $C_*$. Every other one has a different leading
coefficient and loses at most two matches. All $p$ therefore remain
above $T$, and no other quadratic can enter. This proves the exact
three-part list-size spectrum.

The restricted direction $g$ is still nonzero, since no fresh points
were deleted. Thus the $p$ parameters in $\Lambda_*$ give distinct
far received words. Their pairwise ordinary common agreement is bounded
by either individual agreement. For any two distinct parameters
$\alpha,\beta\in\Lambda_*$, their affine mixtures correspond to
$\lambda=\alpha+t(\beta-\alpha)$, an affine bijection of $E$.
The exact spectrum therefore gives nearby probability $1-p^{-2}$
and singleton probability $1-2p^{-2}$.
More generally, uniform $E$-valued weights summing to one on any
$t\ge2$ distinct selected inputs induce a uniform scalar parameter:
the corresponding affine functional has fibers of size $|E|^{t-2}$.
In contrast, $\mathbb F_p$-valued affine weights keep the parameter
inside $\Lambda_*$, where every mixture is far. The challenge field
in the asserted probabilities is essential.

For the threshold comparison, $p\ge4099$ gives $T\ge2p-5\sqrt p$,
while
\[
 n a_1(3/n)\le\sqrt{3n/2}+(3n/8)^{1/4}
 <\tfrac74p+\sqrt p<T.
\]
Using $n\ge2p^2-2p-1$ and $T\le2p-4\sqrt p$ gives
\[
 2n-T^2\ge16p\sqrt p-20p-2>0.
\]
The ceiling in $T$ gives the stated source-separation bound.
A uniform finite first-order certificate is also immediate:
$m=6$, derivative cap three, $B=3T$, $H=40B$ give
$G=4B^2-2B$ and local-rank bound $62$. Since
$29p/5\le B\le6p$ and $n\le2p^2$,
\[
 39G-40\cdot62n\ge\frac{7196}{25}p^2-468p>0.
\]
As $G>62n$, this implies the strict graded row test
$(H-B+1)G>(H+1)62n$, with reconstruction guard $p>3$.
This is an extension-field result in the first-order regime, not
fixed-rate or prime-alphabet tightness of a general upper bound.
\end{proof}
