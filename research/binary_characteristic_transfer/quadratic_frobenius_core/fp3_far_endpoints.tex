\subsection{Puncturing two decoded fibers to obtain far endpoints}
\label{sec:fp3-far-endpoints}

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:fp3-far-endpoints}
For every prime $p\ge4099$, there is a dimension-three Reed--Solomon
code over $E=\mathbb F_{p^3}$ of length $n$ satisfying
\[
 2p^2-4p+1\le n\le2p^2-p-1,
\]
and two words $r_0,r_1$, each with individual agreement at most
$p+2\sqrt p$, such that at least $p^3-p^2$ of their affine mixtures
$(1-t)r_0+tr_1$, $t\in E$, have singleton lists at threshold
\[
 T=2p-\lceil4\sqrt p\rceil-8.
\]
Their ordinary common agreement is also at most $p+2\sqrt p$.
Moreover
\[
 n a_1(3/n)<T<\sqrt{2n}.
\]
Thus the exceptional probability is at least $1-1/p$, with both
endpoints outside the tested radius by an agreement gap at least
$p-6\sqrt p-9=\Theta(\sqrt n)$. The characteristic is
$\Theta(\sqrt n)$ and the alphabet is the native field $\mathbb F_{p^3}$.
The rate and normalized source separation tend to zero.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Use the construction and notation of
Theorem~\ref{thm:fp3-common-agreement}, before puncturing, with length
$N=2p^2-p-1$ and threshold $A=2p-\lceil4\sqrt p\rceil-2$.
Its proof establishes more than the threshold lists: at any parameter
outside $\Lambda_0=(\delta/u_0)\mathbb F_p$, exactly one quadratic
is canonical on both blocks, and every other quadratic has at most
$\max(p+2\sqrt p,16)=p+2\sqrt p$ matches.

Put $u_1=\eta u_0\in W$. Then
\[
 I_{u_1}=\eta^{-1}\Lambda_0,\qquad
 \eta I_{u_1}=\Lambda_0,\qquad I_{u_1}\ne\Lambda_0.
\]
The nonzero core fibers for this direction partition $D_0$ minus its
zero fiber: their total size is $p^2-p$, over $p-1$ choices of $b$.
Choose $b\in I_{u_1}^*$ with core fiber size at most $p$.
The $p$ fresh fibers indexed by $v\in I_{u_1}$ partition the trimmed
$D_1$, of size $p^2-p$. Choose distinct $v_1,v_2$ indexing the two
smallest fibers, whose total size is at most $2p-2$.
These choices are independent. Set
\[
 \alpha=b-\eta v_1,\qquad \beta=b-\eta v_2,
 \qquad Q_*=a_{u_1}X^2+b.
\]
Both parameters lie outside $\Lambda_0$, since their coset
$b+\Lambda_0$ is disjoint from that line. Their unique doubly canonical
witness is the same polynomial $Q_*$. Let $S_\alpha,S_\beta$ be its
full agreement supports at these parameters, and delete
$S=S_\alpha\cup S_\beta$ from the evaluation domain.
This union consists of the chosen core fiber and two distinct trimmed
fresh fibers. The averaging choices give
\[
 |S|\le p+(2p-2)=3p-2.
\]
This proves the asserted length bounds.

Let $r_0,r_1$ be the restrictions of $f+\alpha g,f+\beta g$ to the
remaining coordinates. The witness $Q_*$ now has zero matches at
either endpoint, since its entire support was removed. Every other
quadratic already had at most $p+2\sqrt p$ matches there, and puncturing
cannot increase that number. Thus both endpoints satisfy the claimed
individual bound, which also bounds their common agreement.

Consider any original parameter $\lambda$ outside its direction plane
\[
 J_{u_1}=I_{u_1}+\eta I_{u_1},\qquad |J_{u_1}|=p^2.
\]
This plane contains $\Lambda_0$, so such a parameter has a unique
canonical witness $Q_\lambda$ with at least $A$ matches. Its leading
coefficient differs from that of $Q_*$: the map $[u]\mapsto u^{p-1}$
is injective on projective directions, and $\lambda$ does not belong
to the $u_1$ plane. At a common core match, $Q_\lambda-Q_*=0$, a
nonzero quadratic, so at most two core matches are deleted.
On the fresh fiber belonging to endpoint $\gamma\in\{\alpha,\beta\}$,
a common match satisfies
\[
 Q_\lambda-Q_*=(\lambda-\gamma)g=\lambda-\gamma.
\]
This is again a nonzero quadratic because the leading coefficients
differ. At most two matches are lost on each of the two fresh fibers.
The total loss is at most six, leaving at least $A-6=T$ matches.
Every competing quadratic had at most $p+2\sqrt p<T$ matches before
puncturing, so this list remains a singleton.
There are $p^3-p^2$ parameters outside $J_{u_1}$. The affine bijection
$\lambda=\alpha+t(\beta-\alpha)$ transports them to the asserted
mixtures of the two endpoints. This is a lower bound on the number
of singleton parameters; no exact classification of the remaining
$p^2$ mixtures is needed or claimed.

For the threshold comparison, $p\ge4099$ gives $T\ge2p-5\sqrt p$,
while
\[
 n a_1(3/n)\le\sqrt{3n/2}+(3n/8)^{1/4}
 <\tfrac74p+\sqrt p<T.
\]
Using the lower bound on $n$ and $T\le2p-4\sqrt p$ gives
\[
 2n-T^2\ge16p\sqrt p-24p+2>0.
\]
The source-to-threshold gap is at least
$T-(p+2\sqrt p)\ge p-6\sqrt p-9$.
Finally the same finite first-order support can be checked directly:
$m=6$, derivative cap three, $B=3T$, $H=40B$ give
$G=4B^2-2B$ and local-rank bound $62$.
Since $29p/5\le B\le6p$ and $n\le2p^2$,
\[
 39G-40\cdot62n\ge\frac{7196}{25}p^2-468p>0.
\]
As before, this implies the strict graded row test, with reconstruction
characteristic guard $p>3$. It certifies the first-order regime,
not tightness of a universal exceptional-count budget.
\end{proof}
