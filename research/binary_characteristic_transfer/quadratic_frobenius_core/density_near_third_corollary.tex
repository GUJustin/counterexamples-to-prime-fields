\begin{corollary}\label{cor:near-third-frobenius-block}
For every prime $p\ge4099$, put
\[
 u=\lceil\sqrt p\rceil,\qquad
 n=\left\lfloor\frac{8p(p-u)}3\right\rfloor,\qquad
 A=2p,\qquad T=\lfloor\sqrt{2n-1}\rfloor.
\]
There is a dimension-three Reed--Solomon code over $\mathbb F_{p^4}$
of length $n$, with two received words whose individual and common
agreements are exactly $A$, such that their normalized affine line has
exactly $(p+1)(p^2-1)$ singleton-list parameters at threshold $T$, one
further parameter with list size $p+1$, and empty lists elsewhere.
Moreover
\[
 n a_1(3/n)<A<T<\sqrt{2n},\qquad
 \frac{T-A}{T-3}\longrightarrow1-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}.
\]
The message dimension remains three: $3/n=\Theta(p^{-2})$ and
$(T-A)/n=\Theta(p^{-1})$. The domain is a selected subset of
$\mathbb F_{p^4}$, with no prime-alphabet or practical-length claim.
\end{corollary}

\begin{proof}
Reuse the blocks and classification of
Proposition~\ref{prop:quarter-frobenius-block}.
Write $N=2(p^2-1)$, $m=n-N$, $\delta=m/N$, and
$\ell=T-2p+2$. We retain a uniformly chosen $m$-subset of the second
block. For $p\ge4099$,
\[
 \frac up\le\frac1{\sqrt p}+\frac1p
 <\frac1{64}+\frac1{4096}<\frac1{60}.
\]
In particular $0<m<N$ and $n>5p^2/2$, so $T>A$.
Accounting for the floor in $n$ gives
\[
 \delta-\left(\frac13-\frac{4u}{3p}\right)
 \ge\frac{5p-8u}{6p(p^2-1)}>0;
 \qquad \delta>\frac{14}{45}.
\]
Since $T<\sqrt{2n}<7p/3$ and $p\ge81$,
\[
 \frac{\ell-1}{2p-2}
 <\frac{p+3}{6p-6}\le\frac7{40},
 \qquad
 \gamma:=\delta-\frac{\ell-1}{2p-2}
 >\frac{49}{360}>\frac18.
\]
For either canonical support size $k\in\{2p-2,2p\}$, the
hypergeometric lower-tail estimate used in that proposition therefore
bounds the probability of retaining fewer than $\ell$ points by
$\exp[-4(p-1)\gamma^2]<\exp[-(p-1)/16]$.
Its union bound over all $p(p+1)$ supports is less than one by the
already proved onset $p\ge257$. Thus all doubly canonical witnesses
qualify at $T$, and the same classification gives the asserted exact
list profile and source/common agreements.

The first-order comparison follows directly from the choice of $n$:
\[
 \sqrt{3n/2}\le2\sqrt{p(p-u)}<2p-u,\qquad
 (3n/8)^{1/4}\le[p(p-u)]^{1/4}<\sqrt p\le u.
\]
Their sum is strictly below $A$. The definition of $T$ gives the
strict Johnson inequality, including square radicands.
Finally $n/p^2\to8/3$ and $T/p\to4/\sqrt3$, proving the limit.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
The displayed limiting ratio is optimal under the two numerical
placement requirements $A>n a_1(3/n)$ and $T<\sqrt{2n}$, for sequences
of dimension-three examples with $n\to\infty$ and $3<A<T$.
Indeed $a_1(\rho)>\sqrt{\rho/2}$ gives $A>\sqrt{3n/2}$.
The ratio $(T-A)/(T-3)$ decreases with $A$ and increases with $T$ when
$A>3$, so
\[
 \frac{T-A}{T-3}
 <
 \frac{\sqrt{2n}-\sqrt{3n/2}}{\sqrt{2n}-3}
 \longrightarrow1-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}.
\]
This is optimality only of that constrained loss ratio; it is not a
claim of optimal exceptional-parameter counts or tightness of a DKT
upper bound.
\end{remark}
