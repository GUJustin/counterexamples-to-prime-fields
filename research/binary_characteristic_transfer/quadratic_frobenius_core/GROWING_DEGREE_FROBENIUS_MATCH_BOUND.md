# Growing witness degree: two Frobenius match bounds

This addresses the all-new-witness issue under code enlargement on the
quarter-density domain D0 union D1', with NO neutral padding coordinates. It does not
preserve the first-order placement when the message dimension grows unboundedly.

Let B=F_(p²), and let P in B[X] have actual degree d>2. Any t in B satisfying
t^(2p)=P(t) also satisfies, with z=t^p,

    z²=P(t),   P^sigma(z)=t²,

where sigma raises coefficients to p. Write P^sigma(Z)=E(Z²)+Z O(Z²).
Every match is a root of

    R(X)=[X²−E(P(X))]²−P(X) O(P(X))².

This polynomial has exactly degree d² and nonzero leading coefficient.
For even d, E has degree d/2, and E(P)² uniquely contributes degree d²;
the other product has degree at most d(d−1). For odd d, O has degree
(d−1)/2, and P O(P)² uniquely contributes degree d². Terms involving
X² have lower degree because d>2. Thus there are at most d² matches.

For coefficients over the ambient E=F_(p⁴), restrict a degree-d explanation
to either of the two B-lines composing D0={x!=0:x² in B*}. After rescaling,
the same equation holds. If every coefficient is in B, use the bound above.
Otherwise apply a B-linear functional E->B annihilating B and nonzero on
some coefficient. The resulting nonzero polynomial has degree at most d,
and every match is its root. Hence each B-line has at most d² matches.
The same argument works on the scaled second block, including arbitrary
line parameter and arbitrary puncturing. A genuinely degree-d explanation
therefore has at most 4d² matches on the two active blocks.

Consequently, for any integer D>=3 with 4D²<=2p, enlarging the code to
dimension D+1 introduces no additional explanation above A=2p. All the
quadratic-bank threshold lists and exact source/common agreements in the
quarter-density construction remain unchanged. Its existing simultaneous
quadratic explanations still attain A. This allows message dimension of
order sqrt(p), with rate of order p^(−3/2), but the source and tested
agreements remain of order p and the length of order p².

Critical limitation: for D+1>=4 the old tested threshold below sqrt(2n)
lies below the new first-order curve, since
n*a1((D+1)/n)>sqrt((D+1)n/2)>=sqrt(2n).
This is a growing-dimension tradeoff only. It is not an improvement in the
same first-order regime, not constant rate, and not a practical-domain result.
Independent review of the two-active-block statement passed; the proof is
scoped to the quarter-density domain, not the older neutral-padded domain.


The older length-9p² construction with neutral values f=X³,g=0 is explicitly
excluded: after dimension reaches four, the codeword X³ agrees with every
finite pencil word on all its 5p²+4 neutral coordinates. The active-block
bound cannot be used to preserve that older construction's source distances.

## Primitive-scale variant: degree proportional to the characteristic

This additional variant still uses ONLY the quarter-density domain D0 union D1', with no neutral padding. It weakens exact source agreement to an interval but permits substantially larger message dimension. Choose s to be a primitive element of E=F_(p^4), put eta=s², and choose v outside B with v² in B. The four physical B-lines are B,vB,sB,svB.

Let H have actual degree d with3<=d<=p and leading coefficient h_d. On a core component uB the normalized leading coefficient is h_d*u^(d-2p). On a fresh component uB the received leading coefficient has the additional factor s^(2-2p), so the normalized leading coefficient is h_d*u^(d-2p)*s^(2p-2).

If a core component v^epsilon B and a fresh component s*v^delta B were both coefficientwise B-valued after normalization, their leading-coefficient ratio would force

s^(d-2)*v^((delta-epsilon)*d) in B.

The quotient E*/B* is cyclic of orderQ=p²+1, generated by the class of s, and the class of v has order2. Thus the displayed condition would imply

d-2+(delta-epsilon)*d*Q/2 = 0 modulo Q.

For even d this reduces to d-2, a nonzero integer less thanQ. For odd d the residue is d-2 or d-2+Q/2, and1<=d-2<=p-2<Q/2. Neither can vanish. Hence at most ONE block has any coefficientwise B-valued component.

On that one block, the global polynomial equation between the received coefficient times X^(2p), its constant offset, and H has degree exactly2p, giving at most2p matches across the entire block. Each of the two lines of the other block has a nonzero projected polynomial of degree at mostd, giving at most2d further matches. If neither block is coefficientwise B-valued, the projection bound is4d<=2p+2d. Consequently every genuinely degree-d witness has at most

2p+2d

matches. Arbitrary puncturing of the second block preserves this upper bound.

For p>=257 set D=floor(p/20) and enlarge the message dimension to k=D+1. Retain the original

n=(5p²-1)/2,  T=floor(sqrt(5p²-2)).

Then2p+2D<=21p/10<T. For example

5p²-2-(21p/10+1)²=(59/100)p²-(21/5)p-3>0

for p>=257. Thus the COMPLETE threshold-T list profile remains unchanged: B=(p+1)(p²-1) singleton parameters, one parameter with p+1 witnesses, and all other parameters empty. All newly admitted witnesses have fewer thanT matches.

Both individual source agreements and ordinary common agreement lie in the interval[2p,2p+2D]. The lower bound uses the original simultaneous canonical witness on D0; the upper bound follows from the new witness estimate and the old quadratic classification. No equality at2p is claimed for this variant.

The gap above either source or common agreement is at least

T-(2p+2D) >= (sqrt5-2.1)*p-O(1).

Relative to the capacity margin T-k, its liminf is at least

(sqrt5-2.1)/(sqrt5-0.05)>0.

This supplies Theta(p) message symbols, rate Theta(1/p), and the same Theta(n^(3/2)) exceptional population. It is an extension-field, selected-domain tradeoff. The tested threshold remains BELOW the enlarged-code first-order curve; indeed it is below sqrt(2n), whereas that curve already exceeds sqrt(kn/2) for k>=4 in this low-rate regime. It is not a prime-alphabet transfer, a prescribed practical-domain certificate, or an above-first-order improvement.

The earlier4D²<=2p variant remains useful separately: it preserves exact source and common agreement2p, which the larger-degree primitive-scale bound does not establish.
