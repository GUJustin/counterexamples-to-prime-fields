# A sublinear transfer bound for the F4-linear hyperplane subfamily

September 16, 2026. Twice self-reviewed; exact checks passed. This is
a special case of the general multiplicative-orbit theorem. This concerns one natural linear-sized subfamily of the binary
codimension-two construction, not all binary codimension-two supports.

## Statement

Let V=F4^k, N=4^k, k>=3. Label V minus zero injectively by x_v in a
field E of characteristic different from two. For nonzero a in V let

    H_a={v in V: a dot v=0},

where the dot product is over F4. Distinct hyperplanes identify the
three scalar multiples of a. Let C be a collection of these hyperplanes
whose punctured locators share the first s=N/8-1 coefficients below
their leading term. Then

    |C| <= (4/3) N^(15/16) = o(N).

More precisely, the set A of all three nonzero normals for hyperplanes
in C contains no affine binary subspace of dimension five. Unlike the
O(N^(3/2)) bound for the full codimension-two family, this excludes a
linear-sized transfer within this specific F4-linear hyperplane family.

## 1. Equal prefixes give equal power sums in every characteristic

Newton identities express the jth power sum recursively in the first j
elementary symmetric functions without dividing by j. Therefore a shared
prefix of length s gives shared sums M_j(a)=sum_(v in H_a minus zero)
x_v^j for j=1,...,s. The j=0 sum is the shared support size N/4-1.
This direction of Newton identities needs no p>s hypothesis.

## 2. A binary five-flat forces a short nonzero moment relation

Suppose a_e=a_0+sum_(i=1)^5 e_i*w_i, e in F2^5, all belong to A,
with the w_i independent over F2. In particular none of a_e is zero.
For beta in F2^5 define the normalized Walsh coefficient at coordinate v:

    c_beta(v)=2^(-5) sum_e (-1)^(beta dot e) 1[a_e dot v=0].

For nonzero beta, all its moments vanish:

    sum_(v!=0) c_beta(v) x_v^j=0,  0<=j<=s.

Use the F4 additive-character expansion

    1[y=0]=(1/4) sum_(t in F4) (-1)^Tr_(F4/F2)(t*y).

It gives

    c_beta(v)=(1/4) sum_(t!=0: Tr(t*w_i dot v)=beta_i for all i)
                         (-1)^Tr(t*a_0 dot v),   beta!=0.

For each fixed nonzero t, the five displayed binary linear forms in v
are independent: the trace pairing is nondegenerate and multiplication
by t preserves independence of the w_i. Their common fiber has N/32
points. Thus c_beta has support at most 3N/32; zero is not in that
support when beta!=0. Possible overlaps and cancellation only lower it.

At least one nonzero beta gives a nonzero coefficient vector over E.
Otherwise invertibility of the Walsh transform in odd characteristic
would make all 32 incidence rows identical. An F4 hyperplane has only
three nonzero normals, so 32 distinct normals cannot give one row.

Choose such a beta and let t be its support size. The distinct labels
make the t by t Vandermonde matrix with rows j=0,...,t-1 invertible.
But t<=3N/32<=s+1=N/8, so all these moments vanish. This contradicts
the coefficient vector being nonzero. Therefore A contains no five-flat.

The argument is valid also in characteristic three: some Walsh
coefficients may cancel, but invertibility still supplies a nonzero one.

## 3. Elementary bound for a binary flat-free set

An r-flat-free subset B of a binary vector space of size q satisfies

    |B| <= 4 q^(1-2^(-(r-1))),  r>=1.

For r=1, there is at most one point. For each nonzero h, pair B along
cosets of the line generated by h. The occupied pairs form an
(r-1)-flat-free set in the quotient of size q/2; otherwise their lifts
give an r-flat in B. Thus, with alpha=2^(-(r-2)) and r>=2,

    |B|(|B|-1)=sum_(h!=0)|B intersect (B+h)|
       <= 8(q-1)(q/2)^(1-alpha).

Taking a square root gives

    |B| <= 1 + 2*2^(alpha/2) q^(1-alpha/2)
         <= 4 q^(1-alpha/2).

Apply r=5, q=N to A, whose size is 3|C|. This proves the stated bound.

## Scope and possible next step

The entire F4-linear hyperplane family has (N-1)/3 members. In
characteristic two its punctured locators have the required shared
prefix. A linear-sized odd-characteristic transfer of this family would
have supplied the linear-sized fixed-gap list needed by the paper's
list-to-line padding reduction. The sublinear bound closes that specific
route, including arbitrary injective relabeling.

General collections of binary codimension-two subspaces need not be
F4-linear for one common F4 structure. The O(N^(3/2)) global bound still
allows a linear-sized family of that more general kind. No arbitrary
prime-field list bound or quadratic MCA construction follows here.
