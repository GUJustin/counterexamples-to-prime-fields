The monic degree-2ell locator convolution has 2ell-1 independent rows on
4ell-1 syndrome moments. Its kernel is exactly the span of the two-fiber
parity-check columns. A monic split squarefree degree-d recurrence has a
d-dimensional solution space, generated by its d root sequences. This includes
the root zero with the usual sequence (1,0,0,...). Thus the stated recurrence
plus the explicit domain-root conditions is sufficient as well as necessary.

at most ten distinct Vandermonde columns, fewer than the 2ell-1 available
moments. Their reduced representation is therefore unique. Zero extra-error
coefficients must be removed; counting padded locators as new labels is invalid.

The bounded correction identity also checks. After clearing the two compiler
denominators, the numerator degree is at most n-ell. Removing the two kernel
sets and d0 additional nodes leaves n-ell+1-d0 roots, so the quotient has
degree at most d0-1. Evaluating modulo K_H and multiplying by K_H' J gives

    alpha N_H^r K_H'^2 J L_H' = N_H^t Q mod K_H.

displayed kernel congruence; the second has the required minus sign. J avoids
both kernel sets by its definition. If alpha=0, deg Q<=4<deg K_H forces

nonzero g_H correction. The residual word checks on the kernel coordinates
are correctly retained as separate requirements.
