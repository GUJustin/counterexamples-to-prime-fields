# Weighted Rees presentation and the exact remaining Padé target

## Complete-intersection Rees algebra

Use the weighted first-jet ideal from ALL_M_WEIGHTED_CONTACT_IDEAL.md, with S the degree-n locator and H=Y−g, g=A+BR the order-two Hermite coordinate. Put J=(S²,H). Then

`K_(2h)=J^h`, `K_(2h+1)=S J^h+(H^(h+1))`.

Since S²,H form a regular sequence, their Rees algebra has presentation

`k[X,Y,R,Z,U,V]/(HU−S²V)`,

where U maps to S²t and V maps to Ht. This is the ordinary algebra presentation. It does NOT assert that the initial algebra for interpolation weights is generated by the initial forms of S² and H. Their high-X initial forms can have common factors and produce additional initial generators through cancellation. Those cancellations are the only possible new filtered effect; the unfiltered adjacent syzygies have already been accounted for.

## Exact simultaneous Hermite–Padé formulation

Expand an arbitrary capped source polynomial by Hasse derivatives in Y:

`F(X,Y,R,Z)=Σ_j C_j(X,R,Z)(Y−g)^j`,

`C_j=∂Y^[j]F(X,g,R,Z)`.

The all-m theorem gives the exact equivalence

`F∈K_m  iff  C_j ≡0 mod S^max(m−2j,0) for every j`.

These are simultaneous polynomial approximation conditions over k[R,Z]. All original interpolation weights and joint/R/challenge caps are imposed on F BEFORE this substitution, not independently on the C_j. A basis change that drops this constraint would invent unavailable source coefficients. This is a concrete finite module problem suitable for a shifted approximant basis, or for proving a structural degree defect. It is not another optimization of the declared one-point row counts.

At the frozen benchmark source the number of columns is

`C=13,123,663,101,701,085`,

while the sum of local row ranks is

`R_total=13,125,118,926,520,320`.

A positive dimension ledger needs a total cokernel of at least

`R_total−C+1=1,455,824,819,236`.

But the direct algebraic target is simply rank≤C−1: one nonzero capped Padé solution suffices. A full-column-rank global matrix already has the unavoidable cokernel R_total−C. These two ways of stating the target must not be confused. Solving this primary-source target alone would not remove the separate7.65-percent full normal-ledger deficit.

## Why a shorter linear Hermite coordinate cannot be the answer

For m2, consider F=C(X)Y+D(X)R+E(X), with strict weight<2A and weights(1,w,w−1). At every selected node, the constant contact coefficient forces D(a)=0; the coefficient of tR then forces C(a)+D′(a)=0. If

`2A < n+w`,

then deg D≤2A−w<n, so D=0. Also deg C≤2A−w−1<n, so C=0. Finally E has double zeros at all n nodes, while deg E<2A<2n (for A≤n), so E=0. This is a universal obstruction, independent of the list or word. At the benchmark362550<393215.

The distinguished monic Hermite coordinate H itself has an additional geometric cost. Since B(a)=0 and B′(a)=1, deg B≥n+1 in characteristic zero or characteristic>n. Degree n would give B=cS and force S′ constant on n points, impossible for n>1. Equality deg B=n+1 occurs only for an affine multiplicative-coset locator: write B=S(uX+v), use B′=1 moduloS, and compare degrees to obtain `(uX+v)S′−nuS=1`; after translation its monic solutions have the form S=(X−h)^n+c. This statement concerns H, not arbitrary cancellations in J^h.

## A common Riccati helper cannot exploit a large fixed-word list

Suppose L distinct degree-at-most-w polynomials each agree with the same received word at at least A>w of n coordinates. Assume characteristic zero or characteristic>2w. If a nonzero weighted-contact helper of weight<mA has shape

`F=a(X)Y²+b(X)Y+c(X)+d(X)R`,

then

`L(A−w)≤n`, provided L≥3.

First weighted contact implies `F(X,P_i,P_i′)=0` identically: at each matching coordinate its order is at least m, while its degree is below mA. The actual candidate jet is admissible because `P_i(a+t)−P_i(a)−tP_i′(a+t)` is divisible by t². If d=0, a nonzero quadratic in Y cannot vanish on three distinct graph functions. Otherwise all P_i solve one rational Riccati equation.

For four distinct rational Riccati solutions every cross-ratio is constant: logarithmic differentiation of differences cancels. In positive characteristic, a rational function with derivative zero is a function of X^p; the cross-ratio has degree≤2w<p, so it is constant. Its constant is neither0,1, nor infinity for distinct solutions.

At any coordinate, a value shared by two solutions must consequently be shared by at least L−1: two further solutions outside that bucket would specialize the cross-ratio of the four functions to1, contradiction. Thus a received-value bucket has size0,1,L−1,orL (for L3 this classification is automatic). For its size b,

`binom(b,2) ≥ (L−1)(b−1)/2`.

Summing over coordinates, using total incidences≥LA and pairwise root bound `Σ binom(b,2)≤binom(L,2)w`, gives L(A−w)≤n. At the benchmark n262144,w131071,A181275, this permits at most five candidates.

This is a fixed-word statement. It does not identify candidates at different labels of a received line with solutions of one specialized Riccati equation. Its useful conclusion is narrower: the sought large-list-dependent source saving cannot be supplied merely by lowering the weight of a common Riccati generator. A positive Padé defect must involve higher Y/R structure or a genuinely different use of the challenge parameter.

## Precise remaining optimization

A useful theorem would force a nonzero solution of the displayed simultaneous congruences under the actual benchmark weighted and jet/challenge caps, from a quantified list hypothesis. The generator algebra is explicit, so such a theorem must identify an additional low-weight initial form (a filtered regularity defect), not count universal syzygies again. No such degree-defect theorem is established here; no additional scan was run.
