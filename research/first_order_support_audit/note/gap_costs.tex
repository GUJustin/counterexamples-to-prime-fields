\section{Inverse-margin costs of the older reconstruction bounds}
\label{sec:gap-costs}

The quarter-rate converse also controls the dependence on the margin
above the threshold. Throughout this section, benefits are the leading
normalized quantities in \eqref{eq:benefit}. The conclusion concerns the
specific interpolation and reconstruction bounds in the recovered
September 10 working draft~\cite{DKT}, not the smallest possible actual
lists. The public ePrint~\cite{DKTcurrent} replaces those reconstruction
formulas and proves better inverse-margin powers. Its graded challenge
counts, Equations (63)--(64), also go beyond the uniform challenge count
analyzed here.

\begin{theorem}[Necessary parameter costs]\label{thm:gap-costs}
Let $a=a_0+\varepsilon$, where $0<\varepsilon\le1/2000$, and put
$c_0=(1-2a_0)/8$. Suppose a nonempty downward-$Y_0$ monomial support
$S$ has positive benefits and positive surplus at rate $1/4$. Write
\[
 B=B_{m,1/4,a}(S),\quad R=r_m(\mathbb F S),\quad
 \Delta=B-R,\quad s=|S|,
\]
and let $U=\max_S u$, $V=\max_S b$, and $J=\max_S(u+b)$. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cost-lower}
 m>\frac{c_0}{\varepsilon},\qquad U>\frac m4,\qquad
 V>\frac m4-1,\qquad \frac R\Delta\ge\frac1{8\varepsilon}.
\end{equation}
In particular, $m\ge16$ and $V\ge m/8$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The multiplicity bound is Theorem~\ref{thm:quarter}. Direct substitution
in $31a^2-6a-4$ bounds $a_0<469/1000$, so $a<47/100$ and
$c_0>3/400$. Thus $m>15$.

If $U\ge m/2$, its claimed bound is immediate. Otherwise each total-degree
diagonal has at most $U+1$ monomials, all active in \eqref{eq:rank} for
$\ell\ge U$. For $U\le\ell\le m-U-1$, row capacity is at least $U+1$.
Consequently $R\ge(m-2U)s$. Since $B\le mas$ and $B>R$, we obtain
$U>m(1-a)/2>m/4$.

Apply height sorting and diagonal compression. Neither increases the
maximum second exponent; the compressed support therefore has at most
$V+1$ columns. Its one-sided continuum lift has strictly positive
surplus by Lemma~\ref{lem:finite-lift}. Trimming outside the benefit
triangle improves surplus. Continuum compression and prefix completion
can be performed within the existing width: sorting replaces level sets
by initial intervals of the same measure, diagonal compression never
increases their maximal second coordinate, and completion only adds to
existing columns. Thus there is a positive cap of width at most
$(V+1)/m$.

For $0<w\le1/4$ and $a_0\le a\le47/100$, the cap surplus divided by
width is
\[
 2a^2-\frac12+\frac{1-a}{2}w-\frac7{24}w^2.
\]
Both partial derivatives are positive on this rectangle, and its value
at $(a,w)=(47/100,1/4)$ is $-2443/240000$. Hence positive surplus
requires width greater than $1/4$, proving $V>m/4-1$. Since $m\ge16$,
this implies $V\ge m/8$.

For the ratio bound, rearrange again. Every compressed column has height
$h\le2m$, since positive benefit implies total degree less than $4ma<2m$.
By \eqref{eq:cell-rank}, its rank is at least
$\sum_{u=0}^{h-1}(m-u)_+/2$. For $h\le m$ this is at least $mh/4$;
for $m\le h\le2m$ it is at least $m(m+1)/4\ge mh/8$.
Rearrangement preserves cardinality and decreases rank, so the original
support satisfies $R\ge ms/8$.

At $a_0$, every support has nonpositive surplus. This remains true
when some benefits are nonpositive: deleting those monomials preserves
downward closure, increases benefit, and decreases rank. Therefore
\[
 \Delta\le B_{m,1/4,a}(S)-B_{m,1/4,a_0}(S)
       =m\varepsilon s.
\]
Combining the last two inequalities proves the ratio bound.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Translation-stable nonmonomial spaces]
Let $W\subseteq\mathbb F[Y_0,Y_1]_{\le d}$ be translation-stable in
$Y_0$, in characteristic zero or greater than $d$. Give it the filtered
positive-part benefit $B=\mathsf B_{m,1/4,a}(W)$ of
Section~\ref{sec:degeneration}. Suppose $\Delta=B-r_m(W)>0$.
If every polynomial in $W$ has $Y_0$ degree at most $U$ and $Y_1$
degree at most $V$, all four inequalities in \eqref{eq:cost-lower}
hold with $R=r_m(W)$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The two flat degenerations in Theorem~\ref{thm:degeneration} preserve
the ambient rectangle of monomials $u\le U$, $b\le V$: coordinate
scalings preserve that rectangle, and taking leading parts or limits
cannot create a monomial outside it. They also preserve the total-degree
filtration. Let $S$ be their downward-$Y_0$ monomial limit, and delete
monomials with nonpositive benefit. The retained support $S_+$ still
has downward closure, has benefit exactly $B$, and has rank
$R_+\le r_m(W)=R$. It is nonempty and has positive surplus
$B-R_+\ge\Delta>0$. Apply Theorem~\ref{thm:gap-costs} to $S_+$.
Its coordinate degrees are at most $U,V$, giving the first three
inequalities. For the fourth, since $0\le R_+\le R<B$,
\[
 \frac{R}{B-R}\ge\frac{R_+}{B-R_+}\ge\frac1{8\varepsilon}.
\]
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Sharp margin powers for the older stated bounds]
In either of the preceding source classes, suppose the uniform challenge-degree budget $\ell$ is certified by
\[
 (\ell+1)B>(\ell+\beta+1)R,\qquad \beta\ge U.
\]
Then $\ell=\Omega(\varepsilon^{-2})$. Suppose further that the
reconstruction parameters satisfy
$B_{\partial}\ge V$ and $B_{\rm jet}\ge\max(J,B_{\partial})$, and use
\[
 B_{\rm rec}=(B_{\rm jet}-B_{\partial})B_{\partial}(B_{\partial}+1)
  +\frac{B_{\partial}(B_{\partial}+1)(2B_{\partial}+1)}6.
\]
The unchanged list and MCA budgets of~\cite{DKT}, Theorem~1.1 and
Equation~(23), have orders at least
\[
 \Omega(D\varepsilon^{-3})\quad\hbox{and}\quad
 \Omega(D^2\varepsilon^{-5}),
\]
respectively, where $D$ is the message-degree cap. At quarter rate these
match the margin powers in that draft's upper estimates.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The integer challenge budget obeys
\[
 \ell\ge\left\lfloor\frac{\beta R}{\Delta}\right\rfloor
 >\frac{m}{32\varepsilon}-1
 >\frac{c_0}{32\varepsilon^2}-1.
\]
Also $B_{\rm rec}\ge B_{\partial}^3/3=\Omega(\varepsilon^{-3})$.
Writing $\lambda=(n-D)/(A-D)\ge1$, the list budget contains
$2D\lambda B_{\rm rec}$, and the MCA budget contains
$24D^2\ell\lambda^2B_{\rm rec}$. All their other terms are nonnegative.
These terms imply the asserted orders.
\end{proof}

These are sharp powers for these particular sufficient bounds, even
after arbitrary support optimization or passage to translation-stable
nonmonomial jet spaces with the stated filtered benefit.
They are not lower bounds on actual kernel-vector degrees, candidate
lists, or exceptional challenges. Global constraint dependencies, finer
coefficient-degree accounting, or different reconstruction and agreement
arguments could improve the bounds. The statements above use leading coefficients. For the monomial source
with full coefficient prefixes, the following refinement gives exact
finite-length accounting.


\begin{corollary}[Exact finite-length parameter costs]
Use the source, degree bounds $U,V$, dimension $G$, and local rank $R$
of Section~\ref{sec:finite-length}. Suppose $\Delta=G/N-R>0$. Put
\[
 a_* =\frac{AN-8}{N(N-8)},\qquad \varepsilon_*=a_*-a_0.
\]
If $0<\varepsilon_*\le1/2000$, all four inequalities in
\eqref{eq:cost-lower} hold with $\varepsilon_*$ in place of
$\varepsilon$. Thus the exact uniform count
\[
 (\ell+1)G>N(\ell+\beta+1)R,\qquad\beta\ge U,
\]
requires $\ell=\Omega(\varepsilon_*^{-2})$. The unchanged reconstruction
budgets have orders at least $\Omega(D\varepsilon_*^{-3})$ and
$\Omega(D^2\varepsilon_*^{-5})$. These assertions are uniform in $m$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The finite-length reduction deletes final diagonals with nonpositive
surplus and produces a downward support $S_0$ of saturated rank $R_0$.
Writing $G_0$ for its coefficient dimension and $B_*$ for its leading
benefit at $a_*$, that reduction proves
\[
 R_0\le R,\qquad \Delta_0=G_0/N-R_0\ge\Delta>0,
 \qquad B_*\ge G_0/N.
\]
All retained benefits at $a_*$ are positive. Applying
Theorem~\ref{thm:gap-costs} to $S_0$ proves the multiplicity and coordinate
degree bounds, since its degrees do not exceed those of the original
source. Moreover,
\[
 \frac R\Delta\ge\frac{R_0}{\Delta_0}
 \ge\frac{R_0}{B_*-R_0}\ge\frac1{8\varepsilon_*}.
\]
The challenge and reconstruction calculations are unchanged.
\end{proof}

If $A/N=a_0+\varepsilon$, the exact effective margin is
\[
 \varepsilon_*=
 \frac{N\varepsilon+8a_0-8/N}{N-8}.
\]
For $\varepsilon\ge1/N$, it is $\Theta(\varepsilon)$ as $N$ grows.
At the rounded threshold $A=\lceil Na_0\rceil$, it satisfies
$0<\varepsilon_*<5/(N-8)$. Consequently this framework requires
$m=\Omega(N)$ and $\ell=\Omega(N^2)$ there, and its displayed list and
MCA budgets are respectively $\Omega(N^4)$ and $\Omega(N^7)$.
These remain costs of the specified sufficient bounds, not lower bounds
on actual lists or exceptional sets. The exact finite-length extension
here is monomial; no finite-length nonmonomial degeneration is asserted.
