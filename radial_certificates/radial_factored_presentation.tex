% Optional replacement for the weight-description portion of the radial
% certificate proof; preserves the proposition and its numerical bound.
The weight can be given directly by a short integer factorization.  Set
\[
 D=1{,}884{,}025,\qquad T=341x^2+5y^2,
\]
and define
\[
 W(T)=(851547D-580691T)
       \prod_{i=1}^{12}(R_iD-10^6T)^2,
\]
where the twelve integers $R_i$ are
\[
\begin{array}{rrrr}
734680&2391341&4113168&6361771\\
9106419&12322595&15989086&20095283\\
24650528&29671516&35141871&41012408.
\end{array}
\]
This degree-$25$ polynomial is nonpositive for
$T\ge851547D/580691$.  The exact moment numerator and compact lattice
denominator, computed for this integer factorization, again give ceiling
$5{,}133{,}798{,}314{,}667$.
The standalone script \texttt{radial\_certificates/verify\_factored.py}
expands the polynomial afresh and verifies its numerator and denominator
against \texttt{radial\_certificates/certificate\_factored.json}.
The end-to-end script additionally recomputes every required moment and
checks both this factorization and the original rational-coefficient
certificate.  Thus the factored presentation does not depend on numerical
root-finding for its verification.
